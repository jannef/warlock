# Stats

Developer notes for working on this part of Warlock (moved from CLAUDE.md, #185); the
rules it implements are in spec/08f-stats-and-burden.md. Keep it current with the code, as CLAUDE.md says.

Stats (spec §8 "Stats"; M14) live in `warlock-core/src/stats.rs`: `Stat` (Strength, Dexterity,
Toughness, Intelligence, Willpower, Presence; id, abbreviation, name) is generated by the
`stats!` list there, so **a new stat is one entry in that list** (saves change);
`PerStat<T>` holds one value per stat (serialized as a map by stat id), `Stats` an actor's scores
and potentials (`Actor::stats`, saved and hashed), and `modifier(score)` /
`Stats::modifier(stat)` the D&D 3.5 modifier (`floor((score − 10) / 2)`) for the rules. Content:
`stats` and `potential` tables (by stat id; 1 to 40; missing score 10, missing potential = the
score, never below it; `Stats::from_data` validates) on creature types in `data/creatures.toml`;
the player's come from their character (`docs/dev/character-creation.md`), or from a scenario without one
(`ScenarioDef::player_stats`; the `player` creature may not set any). **Never show a modifier, a potential, or a creature's stats**: presentation reads only
`PerceptionView::own_scores` (the player's scores as the rules read them, raised by magic items
and lowered by status effects, hunger, and stat damage; `own_base_scores` without those
penalties, so the character screen can mark the lowered ones) — the character screen and the `stats` dump layer; only the dev
`actors` layer and `--state` dumps show the rest. Only combat (Strength, Dexterity, Toughness),
burden (Strength and Toughness scores), digging and kicking doors in (Strength), and disease
checks use stats, **always through
`Actor::effective_stats(content)`** (worn magic raising them, then status penalties, Weak, and
stat damage; never `actor.stats` directly in a rule; the hit points a level adds,
`World::level_hp`, read `Actor::raised_stats`, before the penalties, so a passing penalty never
costs them for good), and
no other D&D concept (checks, saves) comes with them. The status bar has no stats segment: it is already
full at common window sizes (it shows `HP 18/18` right after the level name, dropping only
before it, and the burden right after that when there is one).

A background story's stat points (docs/dev/background.md) are `Stats::gift`s: the score and
the potential move together, and a score never falls below 1.

## Training (#231, #722)

Stats grow by use: a signed pile of marks per stat, paid out at checks (spec §8 "Stats":
"Training"; the design is `docs/design/stat-training.md`, slices T1 to T6, #728):

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| `content/stat_training.rs` | `StatTraining` from `data/stats.toml` (`[training]` `price_base`, `price_per_point`, `ramp_divisor`, `odds_power`, `quarter`, `drop_share`, `[[training.use]]` `stat`/`on`/`chance`; validated, a stat and use listed once, `stat` left out exactly for a use that names its own (`StatUse::names_stat`: `drank`, `elixir`, a potion's stat); without the file nothing trains), `StatUse` (the uses, `describe()` for the wiki, `of_skill` for the uses skills share: burdened, kick (a door's and a chest's), dodge, sneak, sneak_attack, spot, study, and, from T2 (#723), Temper → forged, Pick → picked, Disarm → disarmed, Steal → stole, Travel → marched, Appraise → appraised, Forage → foraged; `HAUL_GRAMS`, 20 kg, the `hauled` line), `base_price(score)`, `price(score, potential)` (the ramp past the potential), `drop_price(score)` (half the base of the point lost, never ramped), `price_for(marks, score, potential)` (the rise's for a positive pile, the drop's for a negative one, none at 0), `odds(|marks|, price)` out of `ODDS_SCALE` (65 536): the quarter rule, then the cube, integers only (both scaled to the price, the pile capped at eight times it, `u128`; `odds_power` at most 5 keeps it in range). |
| `world/stat_training.rs` | `train_stat(StatUse)` (one training-stream draw per rule below 100; the player only, alive), `train_stat_times(StatUse, named stat, count)` (a count of marks, a potion's: one draw per mark for a rule below 100, none at 100; Hard training's `TalentMods::train_percent` raises the chance compared, never the draws; `train_stat` is it with 1), `train_fight` (blow, critical (a melee critical of the player's), shot, hungry_fight, hurt, big_hit, block (a blow on the player missed by Shield), parry (missed by Parry): one call in `fight.rs::resolve`), `train_kill` (killed: a melee kill of the player's of a creature of their level or above; one line in `resolve` where `hurt` says it died; `earn_kill` does not know the attack's kind), `train_resisting` (disease checks won: Willpower's → `resisted_will`, else `resisted`), the T2 helpers that keep a hook to one line (#723): `train_spell` (the `spell` use, only the rules of the stats `SpellData::trained_stats` names: damage's weights, Intelligence when it names none, reach's for a spell with a reach (not a touch or on oneself), duration's for a buff or a hex; through `train_stats_of`, `train_stat_times` with a stat filter), `train_wade` (waded: a step onto terrain that freezes and costs at least `WADING_COST` 200, water and running water, unless its ice held), `train_haul` (hauled: a stack of `HAUL_GRAMS` or more among what was picked up, once), `train_hungry_step` (hungry_step: an action, not a wait, while exactly Hungry), `train_shaking_off` (shook_off: each status that ended by easing whose `damage` or `mana_damage` is above 0: bleeding, poison, ebbing), `settle(Check)` (the one rule of both checks: six draws of `ODDS_SCALE` always, on `levels_rng` for a level and `training` for the day; a positive pile rises with its odds, `Stats::train` (the score only: the potential stays), and pays its price, the surplus kept; a negative pile drops with its odds against the drop's price, `Stats::untrain` with the stat damage plus one as its floor, pays the price back (`min(0, m + L)`), and cuts hit points and mana to the new maxima, never below 1, #724; it returns the `Moved` stats, a rise's or a drop's words), `check_stats` (from `World::level_up`, once per level gained; the message into `news`), `daily_stat_check` (the daily check, below), `schedule` (from `World::assemble`: a new run and a loaded save get `next_stat_check`), `skip_stat_check` (the dev skip passes no check). |
| `stats.rs` | `Stats::train` (a rise: the score +1, at most 40; the potential untouched), `Stats::untrain(stat, floor)` (a drop: the score −1, never below `DRAIN_FLOOR` nor `floor`, the stat damage plus one; the potential untouched; `settle`'s drop). |
| `world/stat_deeds.rs` | The losses (#724, design §7): `stat_deed(StatDeed)` / `stat_deed_times(deed, count)` (the player only, alive): each `[[training.deed]]` rule (`stat`, `on`, exactly one of `chance` or `marks`; `content/stat_training.rs` `DeedRule`, `StatDeed` with `describe()`, `deeds(deed)`, `in_play(marks, price)`, the quarter rule) takes `marks` at once (no draw) or a mark with `chance` (one training-stream draw a mark). A deed that takes a pile from out of the drop's play to within it `say`s the stat's warning (`warn_news`); the drop's words are `drop_news`. Hooks, one line each: `daily_stat_check` (hungry_dawn, Weak or worse, before `settle`'s draws), `hunger.rs` `digest` (starved, once per hit point lost) and `faint` (fainted), `eat.rs` `vomit` (vomited), `World::apply` beside the burdened use (overloaded: a `Spent::Turn` action, not a wait or a drop, the burden as the action began), `trap.rs` `step_onto_trap` (knew_better: a known trap sprung by stepping on it), `steal.rs` on a failed contest (caught, by a keeper or anyone; `shop.rs::caught_stealing` stays the keeper's outrage only). Tests: `world/stat_deeds_tests.rs` (each hook, the warning once per crossing and before every drop, the floors, noise cancels, the round trip's price); `hunger_tests` (vomit) and `steal_tests` (caught) assert their marks. |
| The talents (#726, design §9) | one `TalentMods` field each, one read: `price_percent` (`PerStat`, Gifted, `choice = "stat"` (`Choice::Stat`): −15) and `ramp_ease` (Late bloomer: 1) through `TalentMods::price_ease(stat)` → `PriceEase` → `StatTraining::eased_price` in `settle` (and the dev `progress` line); a rise's price only, a drop's never eased; `fasting_marks` (Ascetic: 15 Willpower marks at once at a daily check met Hungry or worse, `fasting_dawn` in `daily_stat_check`, before `settle`); `no_deed` (`spares_deed`, read at the top of `stat_deed_times`: Ascetic's hungry_dawn; the barbarian's Hard living at 18, hungry_dawn, starved, vomited, fainted; the monk's Discipline at 12, fainted); `use_marks` (`use_times`, read in `train_stats_of`: Discipline's endured and prayed count twice). Gourmand and Cauldron carry `stat_training` too (`corpse_stats_percent`, #731). Tests: `world/stat_talent_tests.rs`. |
| `world/train.rs` | `trains`/`train` also ask stats for the shared uses (`StatUse::of_skill`): one report at the hook trains both. |
| The hooks | one line each: `dig_turn` (dig), `after_tread` (plunge, climb onto ice), the pit in `spring_trap` (climb), `expose`/`disease_turn` (resisted), `search_turn` after a find (found), `read.rs` `use_up` (read) and `read_identify` (identified), `earn_kill` before the XP (discovered), `keep_at` ending `Done` after more than one turn (endured), `talk.rs` `chat` (chat; `ask` takes no time, so it trains nothing) and `swap_places` (swapped), `shop.rs` `pay` and a sale (trade), `quest.rs` `complete` before the XP (quest), `World::spend_mana` for the player, a cost above 0 (cast). The T2 paths (#723; design §5): `hold.rs::struggle` after the check (struggled: Strength and Willpower), `travel.rs` beside `wade` (`train_wade`), `carry.rs::pick_up` (`train_haul`), `World::apply` beside burdened (`train_hungry_step`), `surprise.rs::startle` on the player's made check (kept_wits), `train_fight`'s `AttackKind::Spell` arm (aimed), `status.rs` before `ended` on what wore off (`train_shaking_off`), `weapon_active.rs::pay_active` after the stamina (exerted), `daily_stat_check` before `settle` below 0 °C where the player stands (chilled: the mark counts at that check), `cast.rs::cast_spell` once the spell went off (`train_spell`; a scroll's `read_cast` and the class and sign abilities are not this path), `experience.rs::discover_level` (explored), `search_turn` when a turn found nothing (searched; `Use::Search` itself is no stat's use), `pray.rs::prayer_answered` (prayed), `drain.rs` beside `train_resisting` for the player (held_out), `weapon_ability.rs::spend_actions` when the player's Surprised ends (steadied), `class_ability.rs::inspire` and `voice.rs::throw_voice` (inspired), `guild.rs::promote` per rank (promoted), `shop.rs::train_deal` (Negotiation's mark, trade, and bargained when `Effect::Bargain` is above 0), `talk.rs::chat` when the talker was not met yet (greeted). Not built: §5.6's `befriended` (Beast friend's stance is derived, with no moment a beast is calmed and no state for a first time) and `hexed` (the `spell` use's Presence rule covers it). `world/potion.rs` `drink_potion` gives a potion's marks (`train_stat_times(StatUse::Drank, Some(stat), marks)`; an elixir's with `StatUse::Elixir`). `world/background.rs` `story_stat` gives Hard-won's marks. |

**The daily check** (#562): once a game day at 06:00 (`time::DAWN`) each stat gets the level's
check, the same rule (`settle`), drawn from the **training** stream, six draws whatever the
piles, so the stream's position is a function of the days passed and a fight's rolls never
move. It is a moment of `World::live` (`world/status.rs`): `until_stat_check` ends a step at it
and `daily_stat_check` makes it at the step's end, so a week's rest makes seven checks at their
exact hours however time is batched
(`a_week_makes_seven_checks_in_order_however_the_time_is_cut`). A rise is `say`d at once, so
it stops a rest (a rest to dawn then ends "You stop resting after …"). The next check is
`WorldState::next_stat_check: Option<GameTime>`, saved and hashed, `None` (left out of the
JSON) while the content has no training rules, so test content without `stats.toml` hashes as
before; a save without it gets the next 06:00 on load. The dev `skip:` passes no check (it
reschedules to the first 06:00 after it). Quick study of the body
(`TalentMods::potion_marks_percent`) scales a `names_stat` use's count in `train_stat_times`
(potions reach it through `drank`, elixirs through `elixir`).

Elixirs (#568, `world/elixir.rs`) and corpses raise a **potential**: an elixir's trained
rise, the potential lifted to the score where it was below, then `Stats::widen(stat, by)`,
never past `SCORES` (docs/dev/potions.md); a corpse's `on_eat` `widen`, a gameplay draw for
`Stats::widen(stat, 1)` (docs/dev/hunger.md). A corpse's `train` marks go straight into
`Actor::stat_marks` (`world/eat.rs::corpse_gifts`, sure, no draw, no `StatUse`).

`Actor::stat_marks` (`PerStat<i32>`, signed, saturating) is saved and hashed, left out while all
zero (the JSON map of `u16` piles saved before #722 reads as the same numbers). Nothing shows the
marks but the dev `progress` dump layer (`stat marks St 120/160, To -30/75`: each pile with its
price in force); the wiki tables are `xtask/src/wiki/stats.rs` (`stat-training.md` with the
chances, `stat-losses.md` with the deeds and the drop's price and warning point by score,
`stat-prices.md`, `stat-odds.md`, `stat-pace.md`: the expected days at the daily check
alone, worked out from the odds).
Tests: `world/stat_training_tests.rs`, `world/stat_paths_tests.rs` (a test per T2 hook), `content/stat_training.rs`, `stats.rs`.
The pace is measured by the balance probe (#727, `world/balance_probe/training.rs`;
`docs/balance.md` "Stat training"): careers to level 60 whose fights go blow by blow through
`train_fight` and the other hooks, with a sweep of `price_per_point` and `ramp_divisor`; run it
after changing a chance or a `[training]` key.

## Drain (#382)

Points off a stat **for good** (spec §8 "Stats": "Drain"), unlike stat damage, which mends:

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| `content/status.rs` | `Affliction::Drain(Drain { stat, points, check, difficulty })` from an `on_hit` entry with `drain` (a stat id or `"random"`, `stat: None`), `points` (1 to `MAX_DRAIN_POINTS` 5, default 1), `check` (default Willpower), `difficulty` (required, 1 to 40); `OnHitSpec::drain` validates (no `status`/`disease`/`severity` beside it; `points`/`check`/`difficulty` without `drain` are refused). |
| `stats.rs` | `Stats::drain(stat, points, floor)` (the score only, never below `floor` or 1; returns the points taken), `Stats::restore` (back up, the potential raised if passed), `DRAIN_FLOOR` (3). |
| `world/drain.rs` | `World::drain` (one arm in `after_blow`'s `on_hit` loop, after the chance): a `random` stat draws `below(6)` first, then the check (`check::check`: effective modifier + `TalentMods::check(stat)` + `TalentMods::drain_checks`); made: `train_resisting`, `Event::Drained { stat: None }`; failed: the floor is `DRAIN_FLOOR` or the stat damage + 1 (saves require a score to carry its damage), `Actor::drained` counts what was taken, hit points and mana are cut to the new maxima (hit points never below 1: never a kill), `Event::Drained { stat }` (`None` when at the floor). `restore_drained` (from `World::mend`, the temple's `mend` service, `world/service.rs`; a mend is offered with only drained points too). `drained_message` is the player's line, said by `perceive_events` (stops a rest). |
| `Actor::drained` | `PerStat<u8>`, saved and hashed, left out while all zero (`undrained`); the scores themselves are already lowered. The dev `actors` dump shows `; drained St 2`. |

The potential stays, so drained points come back slowly by training and corpses too (those
leave `drained` as it is: a mending later restores what drains took on top of the growth).
Talents: the `diseases` system (`data/talents.toml`; the `drain` tag folded into it, #773): Unbroken soul (`drain_checks`), Iron will
(Willpower checks), Divine grace (paladin, `drain_checks`). Not yet: life (level) drain, a
scroll or potion of restoration, a sustaining affix. The wiki table is `drain-attacks.md`
(`xtask/src/wiki/stats.rs`). Tests: `world/drain_tests.rs`, `content/status.rs`, `stats.rs`.
