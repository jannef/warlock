# Procedural generation and special rooms

Developer notes for working on this part of Warlock (moved from CLAUDE.md, #185); the
rules it implements are in spec/07c-generation.md and spec/07d-shops-and-rooms.md; special
rooms are in `docs/dev/special-rooms.md`. Keep it current with the code, as CLAUDE.md says.

Procedural generation (spec §7, §8) lives in `warlock-core`: `rng.rs` (ChaCha8 streams derived
from the run seed: `map/<level id>` per generated level; in play every rule draws by a key of its
own, `<rule>/<decision>/<actor key>@<second>#<n>` (`rng::draw!`, "Keys" in `rng.rs`: no shared
keyed stream, #1662);
`parse_seed` for `--seed`; `Rng::weighted` the one weighted draw, a single `below(total)` and a
walk, `None` with no draw when nothing weighs anything or the sum overflows a `u32`, which the
loaders of drawn tables refuse with `rng::weight_sum`; `magic::loot::pick_weighted` is it over
`(item, weight)` pairs), `content/dungeon.rs` (branches in the `dungeons` table, each campaign's `data/dungeons/`: the Depths in
`campaigns/showcase/data/dungeons/showcase.toml`; level ids
`depths:N`), `content/vault.rs` (vault rules; authors write to `assets/vaults/README.toml`, which the loader skips, and docs/design/generic-vaults.md §7), and `mapgen/` (`rooms.rs`, `caves.rs`, `vault.rs`
stamping, `connect.rs` flood fill / corridor digging / validation). A branch level is generated by
`World::travel` on its first visit and then persists in `LevelState` like a hand-made level (level
states carry their own ambient and placed lights). Generation is integer-only; a level that fails
validation is regenerated from the next derived stream. A valid branch level then hides its
branch's traps (`mapgen::hide_traps`, from the stream `traps/<level id>`, so the map is the same
with or without them), then a few of its doors (`mapgen::hide_secrets`, secret doors, from the
stream `secrets/<level id>`, only where `connect::validate` still reaches the stairs and every key
without them; `docs/dev/searching.md`). On about one `caves` floor in six a pool has a deep middle (`mapgen/deep.rs`, #1020: stream `deep_water/<level id>`, `Rng::deep_water`, drawn once the cave is built and before `build_walls`, so a floor without it is exactly its old self; a blob of 40 to 70 free floor cells becomes water, those with all eight neighbours in it `deep_water`; kept off creature routes and special rooms, and undone if it would cut any walkable cell off). Rooms floors, vaults, special rooms and hand-made levels never have it. Last of all, after `animated::arm`, a branch floor may get **ore veins** (`mapgen/vein.rs`, #1390; tests `mapgen/vein_tests.rs`): stream `veins/<level id>` (`Rng::veins`), so every other step draws as before; draws: `[veins] chance` of `data/mining.toml`, the count, then per vein its ore (`MiningRules::vein_table` at `Content::floor_danger(id, visit.player_level)` with the branch's `ores`, `pick_weighted`), a start among the `pal.wall` cells, its size, and its growth (a random cell of it, a random cardinal) over `wall` and `void` cells alike, never a stamped cell, the edge, a secret door's cell, or a special room's box grown by one. Only rock changes (`veins_change_only_rock`); veins are the same with secret doors or without (a secret door is no rock either way). The vein terrains are made at load (`content/mining.rs::add_vein_terrain`, `docs/dev/digging.md`); `ascii` draws them `V`. The insta snapshots in
`src/mapgen/snapshots/` and `src/mapgen/local/snapshots/` flag any change in the generator's
layout: review the maps in the diff, then accept them deliberately. They hold the terrain and
stairs (with locks, secret doors, veins, special rooms, shops, and named places; a local area its
start and flow) and leave out what is put on it, creatures, objects, traps, and lights, which
churn with every loot or roster change (#1526); `describe` checks those instead
(`nothing_on_a_wall_in`: on walkable ground, one creature to a cell, chests and traps where they
may be). Loot (`mapgen::place_loot`: floor
items by the branch's `loot`, vault `loot` cells, each a grade or a pool of item classes,
`magic/loot.rs::roll_pool` at the floor's item level, `docs/dev/magic-items.md`) is rolled once a
map is valid, from its own stream `loot/<level id>`, so adding loot never changes the terrain of a
generated level. It does shift what follows on that stream, and `hide_traps` avoids a vault's
`[[pile]]` but not a `loot` cell, so turning a pile into a loot cell moves the floor's items and
may move a hidden trap (a trap may lie under a loot cell). A vault's `monsters` cells
(`content/monster_pool.rs`, `VaultMonsters`; `Canvas::monsters`) are drawn with the population,
just before it, from their own stream `vault_monsters/<level id>`, through the same
`danger::draw`; the population keeps off every marked cell, so a floor with such a vault moves its
population (not its map, traps, secrets, or loot), and only that floor; `hide_traps` is untouched, so
a hidden trap may take a marked cell, which then stays empty (`mapgen/vault_monsters_tests.rs`).
On a floor with no danger nothing is drawn: the Depths' guard post keeps its one kobold. On a
branch with a danger the initial population follows (`mapgen/population.rs`, stream
`population/<level id>`; `docs/dev/creatures.md`: its own folk, now and then a stray from every
hostile type of the campaign, populations or none, unless the branch's `stray_pool` says
`"populations"`), and, from the same stream after its draws, the **leavings** about every creature
it and the vaults' pools placed whose type has `leavings` (`mapgen/leavings.rs`: each kind's
count, `Leaving::count`, on plain floor within 4 cells that nothing claims, no creature, walk,
object, trap, light, lock, chest, shop, or kept-clear room, and off what vaults stamped but
for torn cloth, so a vault's map stays as drawn; a statue only with open ground all
eight ways round, `LevelTemplate::statues` holding a nobody of the branch's population, not
unique or dread, holding nothing (named, as the level is made, from the run's petrified dead in
the graveyard: `world/fixtures/graveyard.rs::name_statues`, one name a statue, the most recent first,
never twice on a floor, which statue drawn from stream `statue-names/<level id>`; decoration,
out of the state hash, `docs/dev/saves.md` "The graveyard"); webs and acid pits change the terrain, torn cloth joins the
pile), then what its vaults' empty chests hold (`chest::fill_vaults`, stream
`vault_chests/<level id>`), then the floor's chests (`mapgen/chest.rs`, stream `chests/<level id>`, one
floor in five by default; `docs/dev/chests.md`), then its treasure rooms' chests, gold, and
gems (`mapgen/treasury.rs`, stream `treasure/<level id>`; `docs/dev/special-rooms.md`; a
greater treasure room is a box with no way in but its breach, which `connect::validate` lets go
unreached), then every creature's gear (`gear::arm`,
stream `gear/<level id>`). Each step after the first lays things only where nothing earlier
lies, stands, or is trapped, and nothing it lays turns a cell with something on it into
terrain that cannot hold it (a chest, a feature, a wall): `mapgen/tests.rs`'s
`nothing_on_a_wall` (objects and creatures on walkable terrain, one creature a cell, none in a
room creatures shun; chests on chest terrain with nothing on them; traps and locks on ground, a
door, or a chest), checked by `every_depth_is_valid_and_deterministic` and its own proptest
`nothing_lies_or_stands_on_a_wall` (#661). Outside generation, a rolled quest draws its target from the keyed
stream `quest/<quest id>` (`Rng::quest`), once, as it is given, never kept (#422,
`docs/dev/quests.md`).

Named vaults (#339): a `[[dungeon.levels]]` range's `vaults` (`DungeonDef::vaults(depth)`,
checked and marked `Vault::pinned` by `Content::pin_vaults` once the branch's campaign is known:
a dungeon vault, shared or of the branch's campaign) reaches the generators as
`Needs::vaults`. Both stamp them first, each on a random spot among every one where it fits
(`vault::spots`; caves also need its tunnels), and fail the attempt if one fits nowhere, so the
next attempt's stream tries again; then the random vault as before. Caves first make room when no
rock is left big enough (#582: `caves::make_room` fills the spot, vault and ring, that takes the
least of the cave with rock, a random one of those, then keeps the largest cave again); without it
Fyrdelf 12, Wulfeshol 4, and Wyrtholm 4 failed all 16 attempts for a few seeds in a hundred.
`mapgen/seeds_tests.rs` generates every branch's bottom floor for 60 seeds and every floor for
20, and requires each to succeed; the long sweeps (bottom floors to 300 seeds, every floor to
100) are `#[ignore]`d and run weekly by the `Ignored tests` workflow (#1514, #1537). `vault::shuffled` leaves
pinned vaults out, so a branch without named vaults draws exactly what it did (no extra draws;
the Depths' snapshots are unchanged). Fyrdelf names the foreman's cabin on 1 to 4 and the
drake's hoard on 12 (`mapgen/fyrdelf_tests.rs`, with the branch's first-level snapshot);
Fennmynster names the sunken crypt on its bottom, 8 (`mapgen/fennmynster_tests.rs`, which also
checks the branch's raised room odds and its lack of shops). The four lairs of the wanted (#426:
Wyrtholm and Wulfeshol caves 100×40, Bānfeld and Brocenburg rooms 80×21, four floors each)
name their criminal's vault on floor 4 (`mapgen/lairs_tests.rs`, with a snapshot of each bottom
floor and a check over four seeds that the criminal is there once and on no floor above).

Ways up (#1117, spec §7 "Ways up"): a branch's `below` (`DungeonDef::below`, checked in
`Content::validate`: a level of another bounded branch of its campaign) makes `leads_down` true on
its deepest level, whose `>` leads there (`DungeonDef::down_to`), and its first level's `<` is
`Content::way_into` whatever it was entered from, as an endless branch's is. The level below gets
one `<` per such branch (`Content::ways_up_from`), placed by `mapgen/way_up.rs` right after the
generator builds and before `deep::lay`: the roomy spot (3×3 free floor) nearest by walking to an
entrance of a vault the level names (`Canvas::named_doors`, recorded by `vault::stamp` for
`Vault::pinned` vaults), ties in row order, else nearest to its own `<`; no stream is drawn, so
every other level is unchanged and only Fyrdelf 12 changed (where its pools, traps, and folk fall). The link
is a plain `StairsLink` after the generator's two, so `World::travel` arrives on it from the
branch above. Tests: `mapgen/way_up_tests.rs` (Ūtgang's three rooms floors and links, the top's
`<` however entered, Fyrdelf 12's second `<` within 30 steps of the drake for twelve seeds and
no other floor's, the `below` content checks).

A bottom (#1434): a branch's `bottom` (`DungeonDef::bottom`, exclusive with `below`, `endless`,
and `portal`) names a hand-made level of its campaign; `leads_down` is true on its deepest level,
whose `>` leads there (`down_to`). No stairs are placed: the hand-made level carries its own `<`
naming the deepest level (`Content::validate` refuses a bottom without one or of another
campaign), so `World::travel` arrives on it, and back up on the deepest level's `>`. Dēopgang's
bottom is Gimmwīc, the deep gnomes' town. Tests: `content/dungeon.rs` (parse and exclusions),
`content/validate.rs` (the validate checks), `world/campaigns/deep_gnome_tests.rs` (down to the town and back).

**Meyn Bew** (#1446; `campaigns/ascmearc/data/dungeons/meynbew.toml`): a pocket of the plane of
earth under Gimmwīc, entered only through the crystal portal Ysella the Binder's quest reveals
in the town (a hidden location of a hand-made level, `docs/dev/overworld.md`), so its first
floor's `<` (the run's `entered_from`) climbs back out onto the portal. Four `rooms` floors 80×21
of danger 22 to 25 (`stray = 0`, `out_of_depth = 0`: earth, greater earth, basilisk, stone giant,
stone troll), a violet `ambient`, no shop, no special room, no ore (`ores = []`). The plane's
crystal comes from vaults (the generators have no palette of their own): `crystal_garden` (crystal
clusters lit red, amber, and green round an uncut amethyst) is pinned on floors 1 to 3, and
`crystal_hollow` on floor 4, with the named place "the crystal hollow" the lure quest's `waits`
names. Tests: `world/campaigns/crystal_guardian_tests.rs` (the portal, the floors, the hollow).

Special rooms (spec §7 "Special rooms") have their own topic file, `docs/dev/special-rooms.md`:
the effects' data, the draw (`mapgen/special.rs`, last, from the level's map stream), the whole-level
version, and what the rules ask of them.

A branch may set its own special-room and shop odds (`room_effects`, `shops` on a `[[dungeon]]`
or a `[[dungeon.levels]]` range; spec §7, campaign design §7.3). `content/dungeon.rs` resolves
them once per depth at load, field by field (the range's table, the branch's, the shared
`room_effects.toml` / `shops.toml`), into `RoomOdds` / `ShopOdds` (`DungeonDef::room_odds`,
`shop_odds`; the shared ones are `RoomOdds::shared`, `ShopOdds::shared`: every kind in file
order). `generate_with` hands the depth's odds to the `Canvas` (`room_odds`, `shop_odds`), and
`special.rs` and `shop::place` read them instead of the content's (the room odds:
`docs/dev/special-rooms.md`). A branch's `keeper` replaces only the keeper's creature type; the
kit is the shared `[keeper]`'s. Fixture branches in `mapgen/tests.rs` (`FIXTURE`) check that a
branch favouring only flooded rooms makes only flooded rooms and `shops = { chance = 0 }` no
shop.

A vault's named places (a legend's `name` and `note`) are stamped with it (`vault::stamp` into
`Canvas::places`, then `LevelTemplate::places`), and `LevelState::from_template` keeps a
generated level's in `LevelState::places` (saved, hashed, left out while empty; a hand-made
level's stay in its template in `Content`, and `LevelState::place` reads both). The Ascmearc's
`hunters_statue` (a worn `statue` with the inscription as its note, the hint of the Hunter's
oath, `docs/design/prestige-classes.md` §8) is pinned on `fyrdelf:11`; `describe` lists places
(`place NAME at (x, y): NOTE`), and `mapgen/fyrdelf_tests.rs` checks it there in every seed and
snapshots seed 1's floor 11.

Which vaults a level may get (#337): `generate_for` keeps those whose `Vault::stamps_on(def,
depth)` holds (a dungeon vault, shared or of the branch's campaign, on a branch or level its
`dungeons` names, if any) on the `Canvas` (`Canvas::vaults`, content order), and
`vault::shuffled` shuffles that list. A branch no campaign vault names draws what it did before.
`spaced_vaults` deals the branch's random vaults round the phases first, then keeps of this
floor's phase only those whose file's `danger` holds the floor's (`Vault::suits`, #1073): the
danger the pools draw at, `Content::spawning`'s rule at the `Visit`'s player level (0 on a floor
with none), read once in `generate_for`. On the endless branch that is the roster's top past it,
not `Content::floor_danger`'s rising number, because what the field guards is what the vault's
pools can draw. Asked after the deal, the field moves no other vault's phase, and draws nothing:
a floor whose list is unchanged draws what it did (`mapgen/vault_danger_tests.rs`).

Which way a vault lies (#959): `vault::turned` draws one of the eight `content::Turn`s from the
generator's own map stream as each drawn vault is tried, then `Vault::turned` makes the vault a file
drawn that way would load as, and the generator measures and stamps that (`vault::spots`, `fits`,
`stamp`, and the rooms and caves code read its size and entrances, so nothing else knows). `ruins` in
`mapgen/local/` does the same. A vault that may not turn (`Vault::may_turn`: pinned, or the file's
`turns = false`) comes back borrowed with no draw, so a floor's named vaults draw what they did, and
`caves::make_room`, which only named vaults reach, works on the size in the file. A level that tries
no random vault draws nothing either; one that does moves after the draw, like any other change to
its stream. Rotation swaps width and height: a floor with little height may not fit a vault turned
across, which is then one more failed try, as for a vault too big. `content/vault_turn.rs` holds the
transform: `turned_template` destructures `LevelTemplate` by every field, so a new field with a
position or direction must be decided on there. It maps terrain, `creatures` (`pos`, `route`),
`lights`, `flow` (`dir`), `objects` (`[[pile]]`, `[[item]]`), `traps` (a bolt's `dir`), `locks`,
`chests`, `secrets`, `places`, `regions`, `loot`, `monsters` (cells kept in row order), `hidden`,
`ice`, `player_start`, `stairs`; `rooms` and `shops` are refused on a vault, and no terrain has a
direction of its own (a stair's `<`/`>` means up or down, a door or wall none). Entrances keep their
row order too. Tests: `content/vault_turn_tests.rs` (a quarter turn against the same vault drawn by
hand; every turn of every vault passes `Vault::new` again), `mapgen/vault_turn_tests.rs` (Flooded
cell and Guard post on real floors, pinned vaults, the draw), `ruins_are_outdoor_vaults_stamped_some_way_round`,
and `mapgen/vault_stamp_tests.rs` (#1072; the full sweep `#[ignore]`d and run weekly, the
default run checking every stamp of 40 seeds and 8 deep ones, #1514): every shared dungeon vault
stamps at least once in 600 seeds of a rooms branch (Bānfeld) and a cave branch (Wulfeshol), some way round, only on a floor
whose danger its `danger` holds (both branches run 6 to 9, so a vault of danger 8 and deeper
meets only their bottom floors); a vault whose `danger` leaves out any of 6 to 9 is also looked
for, in 100 seeds, on Grundlēas's rooms floors and Fyrdelf's caves of dangers 9 to 16 (#1077), if
any of those suits it (one kept shallow, the Tithe-barn to danger 6, meets only the branches'
first floors; every shared vault must suit some floor of the test).
Each stamp has its entrances reached from the up stairs (secret doors count as passable), its
known traps known (a chest's too: the Spiteful Coffer's fireball), the Reliquary's lightning rune
with its find on it, the Delving's pick-axe, the Counting-house's tripwire under its coin, the
Well-house's finds in its wadeable water (#1078), its named places with it (the Four Fonts'
doors), every draw of a pool with the pool's `status` and
`route` (the route walkable and looping), every locked door with a key that opens it lying or
carried inside the vault, reached from an entrance without passing a lock (the Sealed Barrow's
lead key, One of Them Has It's copper key on one of the crowd, the Guard post's brass key), and
every pool's `carry` opening a lock of its vault; a vault with patrols is seen walking them more
than one way (they turn with it). A stamp is found by the vault's terrain and its placed piles
(`piles_hold`: the Wyrm's Bed, a plain room in a plain shape, is told from an ordinary room by
its bones); a symmetric map matches more than one way round, and a stamp passes if any matching
turn holds. A new shared vault joins the test by being in `assets/vaults/`. Its
`old_enemies_halls_are_hostile_both_ways` checks the catalogue's open point for Old Enemies
(#1074): every type the `demon` pool may draw and every type of the `devil` pool are hostile
both ways, since a faction's `hostile` list binds both sides, so no `demon` line on `devil` is
needed. The shallow test level's depths have no danger, so a vault with a `danger` never stamps
there (`vaults_are_stamped_whole_some_way_round_with_their_lights_and_creatures` checks that). Test fixtures at peace
(`world/creatures/fixture_factions.rs::make_peace`) leave the shared vaults out: a tag pool holds only
types hostile to the player.

How often, and how rarely the same one (#960): rooms floors roll `VAULT_CHANCE` 20 %
(`rooms.rs`); cave floors 40 % for one (`caves.rs`), then 15 % for a second. Before it was 50 %
and 1 + 30 %: on the six shared vaults (three stamp in a dungeon) a 600-floor sample of the
Ascmearc's rooms and cave branches met 0.61 random vaults a floor, 184 of 369 a repeat within
its branch, Flooded cell 148 times; now 0.15 a floor, 9 repeats of 89 (the same sample: 20 seeds of
fyrdelf, cealdhlaw, fennmynster, and banfeld). `mapgen::spaced_vaults` (called by `generate_for`) keeps
repeats apart without any saved state: the branch's random (unpinned) vaults, sorted by id, are
shuffled by stream `vault_phases/<branch id>` of the run seed and dealt round `VAULT_PHASES` (4)
phases; floor `depth` offers only the vaults of phase `depth % 4`. So no vault comes within four
floors of itself in a branch, and the deal differs by run and branch. A level's own named
(pinned) vaults are untouched. Adding vaults raises the count a floor may choose from, not the
chance of one.
An endless branch (`DungeonDef::endless`, spec §7 "The endless branch", #1010; Grundlēas,
`campaigns/ascmearc/data/dungeons/grundleas.toml`) has one row of per-depth tables, read at every
depth (`DungeonDef::row`), and `depth` 1 for what loops over them; `leads_down` is always true.
`generate_for` draws the floor's generator and size from stream `floor_kind/<level id>`
(`endless_plan`), and its first floor's `<` is `Content::way_into`, not `entered_from`. The whole
generation runs under the floor's own seed (`world/levels/endless.rs::floor_seed`) with the usual stream
labels, so `warlock-cli gen --level grundleas:3 --seed S` shows what that seed makes, not what the
run of seed S makes on its third floor (that depends on how many floors it has made). Spaced
vaults (`spaced_vaults`) deal anew for every floor under that seed: a random vault may come on two
floors in a row, which no one can compare. `Content::spawning` reads the danger at
`depth.min(roster_depth)`, filled by `Content::fill_rosters` from the highest level of the
branch's population and strays, so every draw (population, dens, hoards, spawns) stops rising at
the roster's top and a floor at any depth holds creatures; `Content::floor_danger` is the number
that keeps rising. The dread draws do not stop rising: `[dungeon.endless] dread` (`content/dread.rs`,
`DungeonDef::dread`) gives `dread_one_in` by the floor's true depth (`Content::draw_odds_at`, which
`generate_for` patches into the `Spawning` the population and dens draw by; docs/dev/creatures.md
"Dread"), Grundlēas's falling from one in 40 at 70 to one in 5 from 100. Other caps past that top: `Content::item_level` clamps at `MAX_ITEM_LEVEL` as
ever; `hidden_at` and `locks_at` saturate and keep rising (the stairs never hang on a lock or a
secret, `connect::validate`). Grundlēas gets its draws by `stray = 100` over the campaign's generic
list and names only the rat as a `population`, which every other branch's strays already hold, so
none of their lists move.

**The Pits** (#1296; spec §7 "The Pits", docs/design/miscasts-review.md §7): a branch with
`[dungeon.portal]` (`DungeonDef::portal`, `Portal`; exclusive with `endless` and `below`, its id,
like every branch's, without `@`) exists only as instances. Hellegeat
(`data/dungeons/hellegeat.toml`) is the one, shared: `campaign` `None`, so it is in every
campaign's fingerprint and `has_level` finds its floors whatever the campaign; its population and
traps are shared, so the cross-campaign checks refuse nothing. A Pit's floors are
`hellegeat@S:N`: `parse_branch_level` gives `("hellegeat@S", N)`, `parse_instance` splits `S` (a
positive `u32`, no leading zeros, so a Pit has one id), and `Content::branch_level` accepts the
`@S` form for a portal branch only and only that form for it (`hellegeat:3` is no level), so
everything that reads `(def, depth)` (spawning, danger, item level, names, arrival, vaults by
`def.level_id(depth)`, `hellegeat:5`) works unasked. `generate_for` builds the neighbours from the
floor's own id (`content::sibling`: `up_to` past the first floor, and `DungeonDef::down_to(id,
depth)`), so a Pit's stairs stay in it; its first floor's `<` is `entered_from`, which
`World::visit` sets to the Pit's record's `from` (its mouth's level, `world/levels/pit.rs`, #1297) however
the floor is reached, a climb from the second included. Every stream is
labelled by the id, and `floor_seed` gives a Pit's floor the run seed (not `endless/<n>`), so a
floor is a pure function of the run seed, `S`, and the depth: `warlock-cli gen --level
hellegeat@7:3 --seed S` prints what a run of seed `S` makes of Pit 7's third floor (but for the
population, read at the player's level as the floor is made, and the first floor's `<`).
`Content::is_endless_floor` is true of a Pit's floor (`is_portal_floor` tells them apart), so
`World::forget_floors` forgets it when left and `EndlessFloors` keeps its `deepest` by
`hellegeat@S`, each Pit paying once; `EndlessFloors::made` counts only the endless branches'
floors. The loader refuses stairs and hidden locations naming a Pit's floor (`Content::validate`),
quests naming one or the branch (`quest.rs::names_level`, a kill quest's `level`, a pick's
`creatures`), and `below` naming one; `fill_generic` leaves a portal branch's population out of
every other area's lists, and `branches_of` (rolled quests) leaves the branch out. Tests:
`mapgen/hellegeat_tests.rs`, `content/hellegeat_tests.rs`, `world/campaigns/hellegeat_tests.rs`; the seeds
and deep-water tests generate Pit 1's floors for the branch.

**Layer bosses** (#1118; spec §7 "The endless branch"): `[[dungeon.endless.boss]]` rows resolve to
`DungeonDef::bosses` (`LayerBoss { depth, creature, vault }`, `boss_at`); `content/layer_boss.rs`
checks each boss unique and each lair a dungeon vault of the branch's campaign with no `danger`,
placing its boss once and marking one `>` (`Vault::down`, the one staircase a vault may have:
`Vault::new` takes it out of the template; `check_lairs` refuses it on any vault no boss row
names), and pins the lair. `generate_for` reads `Visit::slain` (the run's `Quests::slain`; `None`
in `gen` and tests: the lair): for a boss not slain, `Needs::vaults` is the lair alone and
`Needs::down` false, `vault::stamp` lays the `>` as `pal.floor` and records `Canvas::lair`, and the
template's `hidden` holds it as a `HiddenLocation` (id the boss's type, terrain the stairs, `to`
the next floor, name the boss's), which `connect::validate` reaches as it does stairs and
`hide_traps` keeps clear. `world/levels/layer_boss.rs::open_lair` (one line in `fight.rs::creature_dies`)
turns it into stairs. `warlock-cli gen --level grundleas:10` prints `hidden stairs at ...`.

**A Pit's warden** (#1298; spec §7 "The Pits"): `[dungeon.portal] boss = { depth, creature,
vault }` resolves into the same `DungeonDef::bosses` (`resolve_bosses` takes the rows of either
table and refuses a depth the branch has not). `pin_lairs` asks no `unique` of a portal branch's
boss (every Pit has its own warden: `gatewarden`), and asks the lair's `>` exactly where
`leads_down(depth)`: Hellegeat's fifth floor leads nowhere, so `wardens_hall` has none, and the
template's `hidden` is empty (`generate_for` expects `Canvas::lair` only for a boss on a floor
that leads down). For a portal branch the lair is stamped whether the warden lives or not;
`Visit::slain` (from `WorldState::pit_slain`, the warden's id once the Pit is sealed, else empty;
never `Quests::slain`) only takes the warden out of the creatures after the build, so a sealed
Pit's fifth floor is the same map with the hall and a re-rolled hoard. The sealing and the mouth's
closing are `world/levels/pit.rs` (`seal_pit`, one line in `fight.rs::creature_dies`; `close_mouth` in
`drop_orphan_pits`). Fixtures that drop the shared vaults keep the hall
(`ContentFiles::remove_shared_vaults`, test-only). Tests: `world/campaigns/pit_warden_tests.rs`.
