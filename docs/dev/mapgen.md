# Procedural generation and special rooms

Developer notes for working on this part of Warlock (moved from CLAUDE.md, #185); the
rules it implements are in spec/07c-generation.md and spec/07d-shops-and-rooms.md; special
rooms are in `docs/dev/special-rooms.md`. Keep it current with the code, as CLAUDE.md says.

Procedural generation (spec §7, §8) lives in `warlock-core`: `rng.rs` (ChaCha8 streams derived
from the run seed: `map/<level id>` per generated level, `gameplay` kept in `WorldState`;
`parse_seed` for `--seed`), `content/dungeon.rs` (branches in the `dungeons` table, each campaign's `data/dungeons/`: the Depths in
`campaigns/showcase/data/dungeons/showcase.toml`; level ids
`depths:N`), `content/vault.rs` (vault rules), and `mapgen/` (`rooms.rs`, `caves.rs`, `vault.rs`
stamping, `connect.rs` flood fill / corridor digging / validation). A branch level is generated by
`World::travel` on its first visit and then persists in `LevelState` like a hand-made level (level
states carry their own ambient and placed lights). Generation is integer-only; a level that fails
validation is regenerated from the next derived stream. A valid branch level then hides its
branch's traps (`mapgen::hide_traps`, from the stream `traps/<level id>`, so the map is the same
with or without them), then a few of its doors (`mapgen::hide_secrets`, secret doors, from the
stream `secrets/<level id>`, only where `connect::validate` still reaches the stairs and every key
without them; `docs/dev/searching.md`). The insta snapshots in
`src/mapgen/snapshots/` and `src/mapgen/local/snapshots/` flag any change in generator output:
review the maps in the diff, then accept them deliberately. Loot (`mapgen::place_loot`: floor
items by the branch's `loot`, vault `loot` cells) is rolled once a map is valid, from its own
stream `loot/<level id>`, so adding loot never changes the terrain of a generated level. On a
branch with a danger the initial population follows (`mapgen/population.rs`, stream
`population/<level id>`; `docs/dev/creatures.md`: its own folk, now and then a stray from every
hostile type of the campaign, populations or none, unless the branch's `stray_pool` says
`"populations"`), then the floor's chests (`mapgen/chest.rs`, stream `chests/<level id>`, one
floor in five by default; `docs/dev/chests.md`), then its treasure rooms' chests, gold, and
gems (`mapgen/treasury.rs`, stream `treasure/<level id>`; `docs/dev/special-rooms.md`), then every creature's gear (`gear::arm`,
stream `gear/<level id>`). Outside generation, a rolled quest draws its target from the keyed
stream `quest/<quest id>` (`Rng::quest`), once, as it is given, never kept (#422,
`docs/dev/quests.md`).

Named vaults (#339): a `[[dungeon.levels]]` range's `vaults` (`DungeonDef::vaults(depth)`,
checked and marked `Vault::pinned` by `Content::pin_vaults` once the branch's campaign is known:
a dungeon vault, shared or of the branch's campaign, once a level) reaches the generators as
`Needs::vaults`. Both stamp them first, each on a random spot among every one where it fits
(`vault::spots`; caves also need its tunnels), and fail the attempt if one fits nowhere, so the
next attempt's stream tries again; then the random vault as before. Caves first make room when no
rock is left big enough (#582: `caves::make_room` fills the spot, vault and ring, that takes the
least of the cave with rock, a random one of those, then keeps the largest cave again); without it
Fyrdelf 12, Wulfeshol 4, and Wyrtholm 4 failed all 16 attempts for a few seeds in a hundred.
`mapgen/seeds_tests.rs` generates every branch's bottom floor for 300 seeds and every floor for
100, and requires each to succeed. `vault::shuffled` leaves
pinned vaults out, so a branch without named vaults draws exactly what it did (no extra draws;
the Depths' snapshots are unchanged). Fyrdelf names the foreman's cabin on 1 to 4 and the
drake's hoard on 12 (`mapgen/fyrdelf_tests.rs`, with the branch's first-level snapshot);
Fennmynster names the sunken crypt on its bottom, 8 (`mapgen/fennmynster_tests.rs`, which also
checks the branch's raised room odds and its lack of shops). The four lairs of the wanted (#426:
Wyrtholm and Wulfeshol caves 100×40, Bānfeld and Brocenburg rooms 80×21, four floors each)
name their criminal's vault on floor 4 (`mapgen/lairs_tests.rs`, with a snapshot of each bottom
floor and a check over four seeds that the criminal is there once and on no floor above).

Special rooms (spec §7 "Special rooms") have their own topic file, `docs/dev/special-rooms.md`:
the effects' data, the draw (`mapgen/special.rs`, last, from the level's map stream), the whole-level
version, and what the rules ask of them.

A branch may set its own special-room and shop odds (`room_effects`, `shops` on a `[[dungeon]]`
or a `[[dungeon.levels]]` range; spec §7, campaign design §7.3). `content/dungeon.rs` resolves
them once per depth at load, field by field (the range's table, the branch's, the shared
`room_effects.toml` / `shops.toml`), into `RoomOdds` / `ShopOdds` (`DungeonDef::room_odds`,
`shop_odds`; the shared ones are `RoomOdds::shared`, `ShopOdds::shared`: every kind in file
order). `generate_with` hands the depth's odds to the `Canvas` (`room_odds`, `shop_odds`), and
`special.rs` and `shop::place` read them instead of the content's (the room odds:
`docs/dev/special-rooms.md`). A branch's `keeper` replaces only the keeper's creature type; the
kit is the shared `[keeper]`'s. Fixture branches in `mapgen/tests.rs` (`FIXTURE`) check that a
branch favouring only flooded rooms makes only flooded rooms and `shops = { chance = 0 }` no
shop.

Which vaults a level may get (#337): `generate_for` keeps those whose `Vault::stamps_on(def,
depth)` holds (a dungeon vault, shared or of the branch's campaign, on a branch or level its
`dungeons` names, if any) on the `Canvas` (`Canvas::vaults`, content order), and
`vault::shuffled` shuffles that list. A branch no campaign vault names draws what it did before.
The Ascmearc's branches (campaign design §7): Cealdhlāw (`cealdhlaw`, #337;
`campaigns/ascmearc/data/dungeons/cealdhlaw.toml`), six floors of danger 1 to 6, rooms 80×21 on
1–2 and caves 100×40 on 3–6, 30 creatures a thousand walkable cells (25 on the rooms floors,
about 70 in the caves), and its vault the barrow chamber (`campaigns/ascmearc/vaults/`, named by
floor 2's `vaults`: a skeleton guardian with the bronze key, a locked inner room, the silver
chalice of Sister Hild's quest on an altar). Its tests and first-level snapshot: `mapgen/cealdhlaw_tests.rs`
(`describe_in` from `mapgen/tests.rs`).
