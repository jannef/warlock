# Warlock — Affixes for the game's systems: spellcasting, stamina, and the rest

Design document for the owner's review (2026-10-28), revised with the owner's decisions the same day (§14). Written against `origin/main` at 99b15e62 and then against the itemisation change as merged (#621, PR #626: rarity 85/13/2, the affix counts, affix categories). Nothing here is implemented; §13 cuts it into issues. It builds on what is there: the affix system (`docs/dev/magic-items.md`), mana (`docs/dev/mana.md`), spells (`docs/dev/spells.md`), resources and stamina (`docs/dev/resources.md`), weapon abilities (`docs/dev/weapon-abilities.md`), potions (`docs/dev/potions.md`), and the systems surveyed in §6. Every number is data and hidden in the game; the wiki states it (the owner's rule: the game is vague, the wiki is accurate).

## 1. Summary

| Piece | Proposal |
|---|---|
| The shape | an affix is one data file under `data/affixes/` with an `effect`, a `category` (#626), its `bases` (the item kinds it rolls on), a `weight`, and `tiers` by item level; each new effect is one `Effect` variant read in **one** place, the place the matching talent is already read (`TalentMods` style: Frugal caster's hook takes of Thrift, Deep lungs' takes Tireless). No new saved state anywhere: affixes are saved by id, and every hook is a pure read. §3 |
| Tiers | **a ladder of up to twelve tiers** (decision 12): an affix's tiers step up every item level or two from its first level to its last, so a deeper find is a little better rather than a band better; `MAX_TIERS` is 12. New affixes are written on the ladder; the fifty old ones are regraded in the last slice. §3 |
| The item level | a dropped item's hidden level becomes **the dropping creature's level**, at least the floor's (decision 12); today a drop rolls at the floor's depth and only its material follows the creature. Floor loot stays at the floor's item level. §3 |
| Spellcasting | eight affixes on seven new effects and one that exists unused: **of the Adept** (caster level), **of Thrift** (mana costs, every spend), **Mage-wrought** (local: a piece of armour hinders casting less — Warlock has no spell failure; the armour's mana surcharge is the thing to ease), **of the Magus** (spell to-hit), **Baleful** (spell criticals), **Potent** (the damage lift), **of Farcasting** (reach), **of Lingering** (buffs and hexes last longer); later **of Siphoning** (mana on a kill, **within three cells**, decision 7) and **of Wands**. Mystic gains weapons as a base (decision 1). §4 |
| Stamina and the pools | three new **resource effects** that name their pool in data (`resource = "stamina"`, as skill affixes name a skill): **Tireless** (the pool), **of Breath** (its pace), **of Ease** (every weapon active a point cheaper); later **of the Hunt** (a point on a kill, the same three cells). An affix never grants a pool (decision 4). The other class pools get a pool-maximum file each (of Fury, of Steadiness, of Stillness, of Guile, of the Muse): a dead affix on a character without the pool is fine by the owner (decision 3). §5 |
| The rest | surveyed system by system (§6): five more hooks worth an affix each (**Hobnailed** boots kick doors and chests in, **Delving** digs faster, on gloves and on a magic pick-axe through a new `tool` item kind (decision 9), **of Sustenance** slows hunger, **of Wariness** softens traps, **of Health** resists disease), two data-only batches (ten skill suffixes; **elemental weapon prefixes** Shocking, Corrosive, Withering beside Flaming, Freezing, Venomous, with the matching typed resistances, decision 11, §7), and a list of what gets **no** affix and why (temperature feels nothing yet, speed of every action breaks the rest batching, alignment is not for sale, light and luck are covered or unwanted, vision stays the uniques' power (decision 10), negative affixes are deferred to #196 (decision 8)). |
| Categories | #626's rule (an item never has two affixes of one category; every affix of one effect group names one category; a category on both kinds is one group) is the one that lets two effects of one family be kept apart or together. Every new affix names its category here, in #626's naming (`mana`, `mana_regeneration`, `fire_damage`, `cold_resistance`, `stealth`). §3 |
| Talents | no new `[[system]]`: every affix joins a system whose talents exist, read at the very hook. Two talents that make the new affixes matter: **Attuned** (scholar: the caster level your items give counts half again) and **Bellows** (vigour: the stamina your gear gives is doubled). §10 |
| Slices | one foundation PR for the hot files, then hook PRs that ship **no affix files** (fixture affixes in their tests, so nothing shifts loot), then the shipped files in three data PRs one after another (each shifts which affix a seed rolls, reviewed once each), then the regrade and balance pass. **None adds saved state**; none adds an action or a key. §13 |

## 2. The owner's request, and what exists today

The request (2026-10-28), verbatim: *"implement affixes that interact with spellcasting and stamina. also research what other systems we have that made sense we had affixes for."* The issue (#622) lists the systems to read and the examples to consider: mana pool, mana regeneration, spell cost, spell failure, caster level, spell to-hit and crit, stamina pool and regeneration, ability stamina cost; and the rules: hidden numbers hidden in the game and exact in the wiki, one hook per modifier, talents may interact.

**The base from #626** (merged 2026-10-28): loot is normal 85 %, magic 13 %, rare 2 %; a magic item has a prefix only 40 %, a suffix only 40 %, both 20 %; a rare item has 3 affixes 50 %, 4 30 %, 5 15 %, 6 5 %, at most 3 of each kind; **every affix names a `category`** (required in data, `AffixDef::category`); **an item never has two affixes of one category** (`loot::roll_affix` leaves out the categories already rolled; `Magic::check` refuses a repeat in a save, a legend's `[[item]]`, or a unique); `content::affix::check_category` makes every affix of one `Group` name one category (so one per category is one per group, which `Mods` relies on) and a category on both prefixes and suffixes one group; a category may gather a family of effects of one kind (`physical_damage`: damage and extra damage; `stealth`: the stealth effect and the Stealth skill). The shipped names are one effect each as a rule: `accuracy`, `critical_chance`, `life`, `mana`, `mana_regeneration`, `fire_damage`, `cold_resistance`, `strength`, `listen`. Nothing below depends on #626's bastard sword or long sword change.

| Part | Today |
|---|---|
| The affix system | `data/affixes.toml` (settings) and `data/affixes/`, one file per prefix or suffix, read in path order; `content/affix.rs`: `Effect` (30 variants plus the six stats), `AffixDef { id, name, kind, category, effect, skill, damage_type, bases, weight, tiers }`, `Group = (Effect, Option<SkillId>, Option<DamageTypeId>)` (an item has at most one affix of each group); `magic/mods.rs`: `Mods::local` and `Mods::worn`, values summed (Deflection, natural armour, and light: the best); `describe.rs::phrase` the words of each effect; `Numbers::of` the item lists' numbers (to-hit, damage, Dv, Pv, range: none of the effects below show there); no limit on a file's number of tiers |
| Local and global | attack effects on what attacks are local, on anything else global; the item's own numbers (Armour, Protection, Parry, Shield, max Dex, weight) are local; everything else global. `Effect::allowed_on` (by kind, at load) and `loot::may_roll(affix, kind, def)` (by object, at the draw: a range for Range, a Strength for Draw, a breakable missile for Durability) keep an affix off an item that lacks the means |
| Item kinds | `ItemKind`: weapon, launcher, ammo, body, helmet, cloak, gloves, boots, shield, ring, amulet (`ALL`, a fixed array of 11); tools (the pick-axe, `slot = "tool"`, `dig = 10`) have no kind and are never magic |
| The item level | `Content::item_level(level id)`: a branch floor's depth, a hand-made level's `item_level`, else 1, clamped to `MAX_ITEM_LEVEL`. Floor loot (`loot/<level id>`) rolls at it. **A creature's drop rolls at it too** (`world/loot.rs::loot_of`: `item_level(&actor.level)`), and only the drop's material follows the creature's level (`Source::Gear(creature level)`) |
| Saves | an instance is `Affix { id, tier, value }` on `Magic` on a `Stack`, **saved by id with its tier index and value**: adding affixes keeps saves and old replays load; values and tiers are checked on load (`Magic::check`). Affixes are not in the content fingerprint |
| Mana | `Actor::max_mana` reads `MaxMana` (Mystic), `World::mana_rate` reads `ManaRegeneration` (of Clarity) beside `TalentMods::mana_regen_percent`; `Actor::mana_cost` applies `TalentMods::mana_cost` (Frugal caster, −20 %), the one cut every spend goes through (`World::spend_mana`: spells, rods, Inspire, summons, the dev drain) |
| Spells | `Actor::caster_level` already reads `Effect::CasterLevel` ("no affix file grants it yet"); `Actor::spell_base` (the tier price × armour × the talents' percents), `Actor::spell_cost` = `spell_base` through `mana_cost`; `Actor::armour_spell_cost` sums the worn pieces' `spell_cost_percent` (chain 10, scale 20, banded 30, plate 40, great helm 10, large shield 10, tower shield 20; eight objects) less Battle caster's ease; `Actor::spell_to_hit`, `spell_lift` (Keen mind's `more`), `spell_reach` (School focus's cell), `spell_degrees_more` (`world/spell_scale.rs`, `world/cast.rs`); an own cast's attack is `Profile::bare(Attack { to_hit, threat 22, crit ×2 }).with(benefit)`, the roll held to the cap; **there is no spell failure**: the magic design made armour a cost, never a chance |
| Stamina | the last resource in `data/resources.toml`: max 10 + the Toughness modifier + a point per 10 levels (at least 5), back a point every 12 s, resting too; granted by Trained wind (seven classes) or by the first active rank learned. `Actor::resource_max` adds `TalentMods::resource_max` (Deep lungs +4); `World::resource_rate` adds `resource_rate` (Steady breathing +50 %); `Actor::weapon_stamina` takes `weapon_stamina` (Drillmaster −1) and `ability_stamina` (Armour-breaker) off an active's cost, at least 1; `World::gain_resource` adds the data's `builds` and the talents' `resource_builds` (Second breath); `resource::pass` clamps a pool to its maximum as time moves. The other pools: rage (barbarian), focus (ranger), openings (thief), ki (monk), inspiration (bard), each on the same hooks |
| Damage types | `data/damage_types.toml`: slashing, piercing, bludgeoning (physical: Protection soaks them), fire, cold, electricity, acid, poison, force, necrotic (elements: armour never soaks them). Elemental weapon prefixes exist for fire (Flaming), cold (Freezing), and poison (Venomous); resistance suffixes for fire (of the Salamander) and cold (of Warmth) |
| Creatures | mana affixes on creature gear work (#525: a goblin wizard's Mystic amulet); creatures never hold resources; creature spells are announced only; creatures never kick doors or dig |
| Talents | the systems a talent may name (`data/talents.toml`): `mana`, `spells`, `resources`, `stamina`, `magic_items`, `damage_types`, and the rest; every system below the bar has at least two talents already |

## 3. What a new affix is: the shape every candidate takes

Every candidate in §4 to §7 is described by the same things, so the implementer has nothing to decide but the code:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Name, kind** | the prefix ("Baleful") or suffix ("of Thrift"), in the register the affixes already use: one plain word, or "of the Animal", or "of a Quality". Names and the refactors they need are the implementer's (decision 13): the better design over the cheaper one |
| **Effect** | an id in `Effect` (`content/affix.rs`), new ones marked; a new effect is one variant, one `Mods::add` arm (sum unless said), one phrase in `describe.rs`, one word in `xtask/src/wiki/magic.rs` (`effect_words`, `tier_value`), one `allowed_on` arm where a kind is the means, one `may_roll` arm where the object is |
| **Category** | #626's, named here for every new affix in its naming (one effect, one category, named for the effect; a family only where stacking two effects would be one bonus twice) |
| **Bases** | the item kinds (`weapon`, `launcher`, `ammo`, `body`, `helmet`, `cloak`, `gloves`, `boots`, `shield`, `ring`, `amulet`, and the new **`tool`**, decision 9) and so the slots: Main or Off hand, Missile weapon, Missiles, Body, Head, Cloak, Hands, Feet, a hand (shield), Left or Right ring, Neck, Tool |
| **Weight** | how often it rolls among those that may (100 the default; 60 for a secondary one, 40 for a rare and strong one, as Brutal and of Ruin) |
| **Tiers: the ladder** | decision 12: **at most twelve tiers** (`MAX_TIERS = 12`, a loader check), **appearing gradually**: a tier every item level or two from the affix's *first* level to its *last*, the values stepping evenly from the first tier's range to the last's (the range widening a point or two as it climbs where the value is a flat number). Each row below gives the **first and the last** (level: value); the file lists every step between. The Ascmearc's floors run 1 to 12 (Cealdhlāw 1–6, Fennmynster to 8, Fyrdelf to 12); an affix's last tier may sit at 13 or 14 for deeper places. The fifty existing affixes (2 to 4 tiers each, mostly at 1/3–4/5/7–8) are regraded to the ladder in X1 |
| **The one hook** | the function that reads it, always the one that reads the matching talent; the affix's value joins the talent's term there and nowhere else |
| **Local or global** | global unless said: none of the new effects is an attack effect; Mage-wrought is the one local one (it changes its own piece); the elemental prefixes of §7 are attack effects, local as Flaming is |

Values are positive numbers meaning "more" or "less" as the effect says (as Featherweight's `weight` is "N % lighter" and of Celerity's "N % faster"), never signed. Percent effects that stack with a talent's are capped where speeds are (50 off in all); flat ones add. Everything applies unidentified, as every affix does.

**The item level of a drop** (decision 12): a creature's drop rolls at **the dropping creature's level, at least the floor's item level** (`loot_of`: `max(item_level(floor), creature.level)`, clamped to `MAX_ITEM_LEVEL`), so an out-of-depth creature drops a deeper item and a kobold on Fyrdelf 10 a floor-level one. Floor loot keeps the floor's item level (a generated branch's depth, which is the danger it is planned at; a hand-made level's `item_level`). The hidden level is never shown. This is F1's (§13): it moves every creature drop's roll on a floor where the creature's level is above the depth, so the snapshots that hold such drops change once.

Mystic (maximum mana) and of Clarity (mana regeneration) stay; the issue's "mana pool" and "mana regeneration" are them. **Mystic gains `weapon` as a base** (decision 1: the staff in a caster's hand; weight unchanged). The rest of the issue's list, with the one candidate each, and what else the spell system offers:

**Considered and left out.** *Mana resting pace* (Meditation's hook): too narrow beside of Clarity. *Overreach cost*: a talent's niche. *Spell cost by school* (School focus): the per-tag machinery would need a tag in the `Group`; one of Thrift covers it. *A critical multiplier for spells*: see Baleful (decision 5). *Casting while Raging*: a status block is a rule, not a number. *Spell failure chance*: none exists and the magic design chose not to have one; Mage-wrought is the affix of that decision.

**Interactions with the talents** (each at the same hook, so they add and the cap holds): Frugal caster + of Thrift (−20 and −16 is −36; two rings and the talent hit the 50 cap); Arcane training + of the Adept; Keen mind + Potent (both widen `max_lift`); School focus + of Farcasting (two cells past the base, never the cap); Archmage's −50 % multiplies with of Thrift's cut, each applied where it is today.

## 5. Stamina affixes, and the resource effects

Stamina is a resource (`docs/dev/resources.md`), so its effects are written **per resource** and name their pool in data, exactly as a skill affix names its skill: `effect = "resource_max"`, `resource = "stamina"`. `Group` grows a fourth member, the resource (`Group { effect, skill, damage_type, resource }`, a struct now), so "one affix per effect" means one per (effect, resource), and `check_category` keys on it. The loader refuses an unknown resource and a resource effect without one.

| Affix | Effect (id) | Category | Bases | Weight | Ladder (first → last) | The one hook | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tireless** (prefix) | `resource_max` (new) | `stamina` | body, gloves, boots, ring, amulet | 60 | 1: +1 → 12: +5 (+1 at 1, +1–2 at 3, +2 at 5, +2–3 at 7, +3 at 9, +3–4 at 11, +4–5 at 12) | `Actor::resource_max`: `more` = the talents' (Deep lungs +4) + the worn items' for that resource | a pool of 10 to 14 at the Ascmearc's levels; +3 is a lunge more. The pool is clamped as time moves (`resource::pass`); a new `World::keep_resources_within_max` beside `keep_mana_within_max` makes taking the item off exact at once (one call in `World::apply`) |
| **of Breath** (suffix) | `resource_rate` (new: N % faster) | `stamina_pace` | body, helmet, cloak, amulet | 60 | 1: 10–15 % → 12: 50–60 % (a step every two levels) | `World::resource_rate`: 100 + the talents' (Steady breathing +50) + the items'; never below 0. The function's early return on `talents.is_empty()` becomes "no talents and nothing worn" | resting's 0 for a pool that does not refill while resting stands (stamina refills resting, so it is moot for it) |
| **of Ease** (suffix) | `active_stamina` (new: every weapon active costs N stamina less) | `stamina_cost` | weapon, launcher, shield, gloves | 40 | 3: 1 → 10: 2 | `Actor::weapon_stamina`: `own − weapon_stamina − named − items`, at least 1; the early return on `talents.is_empty()` goes the same way | **global** (decision 2), not local: it is no attack effect; the actives one may use are those of what one holds anyway, so a local reading would give the same answer with more code. Drillmaster's −1 and of Ease's −2 bring a 3-stamina active to the floor of 1 |
| **of the Hunt** (suffix; E1) | `resource_on_kill` (new) | `stamina_on_kill` | weapon, launcher, boots | 40 | 3: 1 → 10: 1–2 | `fight.rs::resolve`, the same line as of Siphoning's: `gain_resource(by, Kill)` stays in `dies` for the data's `builds` and the talents' (Second breath, any kill); the affix's point is added beside the siphon, under the same limit | **the same limit as of Siphoning** (a kill by one's own blow, shot, or spell within three cells; decision 7 read onto its twin, so the two on-kill affixes have one rule and one constant) |
| **of Fury** (rage), **of Steadiness** (focus), **of Stillness** (ki), **of Guile** (openings), **of the Muse** (inspiration): suffixes | `resource_max` with that `resource` | `rage`, `focus`, `ki`, `openings`, `inspiration` | ring, amulet, body | 40 | rage 2: +2 → 12: +8; focus 2: +1 → 12: +4; ki 2: +1 → 12: +3; openings 2: +1 → 12: +3; inspiration 2: +1 → 12: +3 (steps every two or three levels) | `Actor::resource_max`, the same hook | **decision 3**: the owner does not mind dead loot, so the pools get their file each with nothing clever (no weighting by what the player holds). The pool's maximum only: its pace and costs are each class's own talents' business. Dead on a character without the pool; the description says what it does and the wiki which class holds the pool |

**An affix never grants a pool** (decision 4). One who holds no stamina (a wizard, a priest) gets nothing from Tireless; the description still says what it does, and the wiki says who holds stamina. The alternative, an item that grants the pool while worn, would need an un-grant on removal (a fourth grant place, and a pool that comes and goes with a ring), and the mana affixes set the precedent the other way: Mystic raises a maximum, it never makes a caster. Creatures never hold pools, so resource affixes on creature gear do nothing for the creature and everything for the one who picks them up; the dev `actors` layer shows the gear as it is.

## 7. The data-only batches: skill suffixes, elemental weapons, typed resistances

Files only; no code, no new effect. Three existing effects take them: `skill` (five suffixes today), `elemental` (three prefixes), and `resistance` (two suffixes). Written on the ladder (§3).

**Skill suffixes** (C1; `effect = "skill"`, category the skill's id as #626 names them, weight 60; the ladder 1: 8–12 → 12: 45–50 for the value skills, 1: 4–6 → 12: 28–32 for the gated ones as of Shadows'): **of Seeking** (search; helmet, ring, amulet), **of the Eagle** (spot; helmet, amulet), **of the Stag** (athletics: kicks and speed; boots, body), **of the Leech** (heal, the physician's old name; gloves, ring), **of the Picklock** (open_lock; gloves, ring), **of Disarming** (disable_device; gloves), **of Appraisal** (appraise: the shops' bargain; helmet, ring, amulet), **of the Scholar** (spellcraft; helmet, ring, amulet), **of Letters** (decipher_script; helmet, ring), **of the Cutpurse** (steal; gloves, cloak): ten. Skills whose benefit nothing reads yet (swim, concentration, escape artist, intimidate, knowledge, music, metalcraft, woodcraft, bluff, handle animal) get none until they do; C1's test holds every shipped skill suffix to a skill with a benefit in `data/skills.toml`.

**The gate.** A skill affix adds to the skill's value "for what that skill gives, trained or not", but Spot's noticing in passing, Open lock's Pick lock, Disable device's Disarm, Heal's treating, Steal's stealing, and Survival's foraging are gated on the skill being **trained at all** (`Skills::trains_effect`). So of the Eagle, of the Picklock, of Disarming, of the Leech, and of the Cutpurse help the trained only (a better check), and a thief finds them; the untrained get a value nothing reads. The wiki's skills table marks the gated skills, and the suffix row says "for those trained in it". No code changes this; it is the skill system's rule.

**Elemental weapon prefixes** (W1, decision 11; `effect = "elemental"`, local on weapon, launcher, ammo, as Flaming; category `<type>_damage`; the ladder 2: 1d2 → 12: 1d8 in the dice's sides, as Flaming's 2–3 to 6–8 spread over six steps): **Shocking** (electricity; weight 50), **Corrosive** (acid; weight 40), **Withering** (necrotic; weight 30, weapon and ammo only: a withering bow is no bow). Fire, cold, and poison have theirs. **Force** gets none: it is the spell's kind (Magic missile, Arcane bolt), and a blade of force reads as nothing; it stays a resistance only. The elements are the damage types without `physical`; the loader already refuses an elemental affix of a physical type. W1 also regrades Flaming, Freezing, and Venomous to the same ladder, so the six read as one family in the wiki.

**Typed resistances** (W1; `effect = "resistance"` with a `damage_type`, category `<type>_resistance` as #626 names of Warmth's `cold_resistance`; the ladder 1: 8–10 % → 12: 42–45 %, capped at 75 with everything worn): **of the Adder** (poison; body, cloak, ring, amulet: the poisoned status meets it as a dose, so a Venomous blade's rider and a centipede's bite are both softened), **of the Storm** (electricity; cloak, shield, ring, amulet: lightning traps and Call lightning), **of Glass** (acid; body, gloves, boots, amulet), **of the Anvil** (force; shield, ring, amulet: Magic missile, Arcane bolt), **of the Living** (necrotic; body, amulet: Cause wounds, Blight). The physical kinds get none: Protection is their resistance.

## 12. Choices made along the way

| Choice | Why |
|---|---|
| Mana cost is one global cut (`mana_cost`), not a spell cost and a rod cost | every spend goes through `Actor::mana_cost` already; one hook, one affix, one category |
| Mage-wrought is local and rolls only on hindering pieces | the surcharge is the piece's; an affix that could roll on a cloth robe would be nothing |
| Spell to-hit and threat reach own casts only | scrolls and wands cast with the reader's Dexterity by the magic design's choice; the scales set the pattern |
| No spell critical multiplier | the cap holds a critical's roll; the multiplier would be a lie on the label (decision 5) |
| Resource effects name their pool in data | the skill affixes' pattern; stamina and the five pools, any later one without code |
| No affix grants a pool | Mystic's precedent; a grant on wear needs an un-grant on removal (decision 4) |
| The class pools get a maximum each and nothing clever | the owner does not mind dead loot (decision 3) |
| of Ease is global | no attack effect; a local reading would answer the same (decision 2) |
| The on-kill affixes count a kill of one's own within three cells | the owner asked for a limit "like range" (decision 7); one rule for both affixes |
| A ladder of up to twelve tiers, drawn from a window near the item level | the owner's "gradually appear" (decision 12); a flat draw over twelve tiers would make depth meaningless |
| A drop's level is the creature's, at least the floor's | the owner's "hidden ilvl equal to the dropping creature or the floor" (decision 12); the floor's stays for floor loot |
| Skill suffixes for the gated skills too | a thief with Open lock trained is exactly who finds of the Picklock useful; the wiki says who it helps |
| No magic-find affix | floor loot is rolled at level creation; a ring swapped on the stairs is a loop the owner did not ask for |
| No all-action speed | the rest batching's exactness rests on creatures never being hastened |
| A `tool` item kind, and a magic pick-axe in loot | the owner (decision 9); the one object today, the kind ready for more |
| A foundation PR for the hot files, then hook PRs with no files, then the files one PR at a time | the `Effect` list and its readers are one file each; every shipped file shifts loot rolls, so the churn is paid once per data PR and never in parallel (decision 14: avoid conflicts) |
| Two talents under existing systems, no new tag | affixes extend `magic_items`; the bar is met; the talents give the gear a reason to be sought |
