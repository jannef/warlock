# Starting kit

Nobody sets out empty-handed. A new character starts with a **kit** that comes from who they
are: what their [class](classes.md) carries, then a few things of their [race](races.md). The
summary at the end of character creation lists it, item by item, and it is in your
[inventory](objects.md#your-pack) and on the [equipment](equipment.md) screen from the first [turn](time.md#what-takes-time): weapons
in hand, armour on, [missiles](ranged.md#getting-ready) quivered, a [dwarf](races.md#race-dwarf)'s pick-axe ready in the Tool [slot](equipment.md#what-fits-where).

Kits are not fair, and are not meant to be. The [aristocrat](classes.md#aristocrat) sets out with a fine rapier, an
heirloom ring, furs, a [jewel](gems.md), and a heavy purse; the [merchant](classes.md#merchant) with goods to sell and [gems](gems.md) to
trade; the [commoner](classes.md#commoner) with a stick and a turnip.

## How it works

- **Wielded and worn**: what the kit wields or wears goes into the first free [slot](equipment.md#what-fits-where) it fits, as if
  you had put it on yourself. A second light weapon goes to your [off hand](fighting-styles.md#two-weapons) (see
  [Fighting styles](fighting-styles.md)); what finds no free slot stays in your [pack](objects.md#your-pack).
- **It follows your training**: some things come only with a [weapon skill](weapon-skills.md) or
  [fighting style](fighting-styles.md) your class trained you in. A [barbarian](classes.md#barbarian) who learned [axes](weapon-skills.md#weapon-skill-axes)
  carries a great axe, one who learned [great blades](weapon-skills.md#weapon-skill-great-blades) a great sword; a [thief](classes.md#thief) of [Twin Blades](fighting-styles.md#style-twin-blades)
  wields two daggers, a [Duelist](fighting-styles.md#style-duelist) one, with a stiletto up the sleeve.
- **One of a few**: where a kit says "one of", one of them is picked when your character is made,
  as your stats are rolled. The same character in the same run always gets the same.
- **Magic**: a few kits hold something that may be magic ("perhaps magic"), or surely is ("magic
  or rare"). Like anything you find, it is [unidentified](magic-items.md#unidentified-items) until you learn what it does (see
  [Magic items](magic-items.md)); you can tell its [rarity](magic-items.md#rarity) at once.
- **Food** is fresh when you set out (see [Food](food.md)).
- **Weight**: nobody starts [strained](burden.md#burden-1) under their kit, and only wealth weighs anybody down: a weak
  [aristocrat](classes.md#aristocrat) may stagger under that purse, and a small [merchant](classes.md#merchant) under their wares. See
  [Burden](burden.md).
- **Your [birth sign](horoscope.md#what-a-sign-does)** gives nothing yet.

## Scenarios

Every run you start from **New run** starts you with your kit, and nothing else: the
[Ascmearc](ascmearc.md)'s start gives you nothing on top. The summary lists it all before you
start.
