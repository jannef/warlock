# Familiars

A **familiar** is the [witch](classes.md#witch)'s small beast of the old craft. It is hers from the
first turn, through her [Familiar](classes.md#talent-familiar) (level 1): a cat (*catt*), a toad
(*tādige*), a raven (*hræfn*), a hare (*hara*), a bat (*hrēremūs*), or another small beast from her
race's list. She has one at a time. She may also keep a [companion](companions.md#companions)
beside it; they are different things.

A familiar is a **kept** beast, under the same rules as a companion:

- It wears a [collar](companions.md#the-collar).
- It has a name, drawn from those the English witch trials recorded ("Tibb, your cat, winds about
  your ankles.").
- It stays until it is slain, and its level is hers ([growth](companions.md#growth)).
- It [always comes along](companions.md#companions).
- It dies as a companion does, leaving a corpse.

It obeys the [orders](allies.md#orders), and its kills pay as any [ally](allies.md)'s do. It is not
a fighter. Its blow is a scratch; what the blow carries is the hex.

## The touch

When a familiar's melee blow hits a creature and leaves it alive, the creature is
[hexed](status-effects.md#status-ill-wished) as if the witch had cast
[Evil eye](magic.md#spell-evil-eye) on it. The hex has the degrees she would cast it at now, and
costs no [mana](mana.md) and none of her turns.

**She is the hex's giver**, so anything that works on a hex of hers works on this one. Her
[cackle](abilities.md#ability-cackle) keeps it on, and her [malison](classes.md#talent-malison)
bites through it. The same touch also starts every other [hex](status-effects.md) of hers on that
creature afresh at its own degree: while the familiar scratches, her hexes stick. No other ally's
blow does this, a cat's included. The touch is a familiar's alone.

## What it goes for

A familiar **goes for what she has hexed**. The nearest creature it sees under a hex of hers,
within its [order](allies.md#orders)'s reach, comes before any other, however much nearer the
others stand. With nothing hexed in sight it fights as any ally does, and its first hit hexes what
it bit.

## Call familiar

A familiar stays until it is slain. When hers is dead, she may call another with
[Call familiar](abilities.md#ability-call-familiar), which comes with her Familiar. It is **an
hour's sitting** and costs nothing but the hour: no [mana](mana.md), no gold, no cooldown, and no
limit on how often.

It is refused, for no time:

- while her familiar lives, wherever it is;
- with a hostile in her sight;
- while a [status](status-effects.md) keeps her from casting
  ([Silenced](status-effects.md#status-silenced), [Raging](status-effects.md#status-raging)).

A creature coming into view, a blow, or any news breaks the sitting off. The time spent is spent,
and she may begin again. At its end **the familiar she lost comes back**: the same kind of beast
with the same name, grown to her level now. "Tibb, your cat, comes back to you out of the dark."
With nowhere near her for it to stand, nothing comes and the hour is lost. On the overworld it
joins her at once and steps out at the next place.

So a calling never brings a different beast. Only a witch who never had a familiar draws one
when she calls: "Something comes out of the dark and sits at your feet.", a beast drawn as at the
start, which is hers from then on. From level 30 of her class, that draw may answer with an
[imp](creatures.md#creature-imp), one more beast on her race's list. It stings and it poisons, and
its touch is the same evil eye.

## Familiar's sight

From level 25, the witch's **[Familiar's sight](classes.md#talent-familiars-sight)** lets her share
what her familiar has. It works while the familiar stands on the same level, not while it rides with
her across the overworld.

This is the [shared sight](companions.md#hunters-bond) of Hunter's bond, by the same rules. She
sees every cell it sees, by its own eyes. She also sees the cell of every foe its nose finds,
through the dark and even a **closed door**, but never a wall. For her, a familiar's nose reaches at
least as far as the rules below say, even for a kind with no nose of its own; it still hunts by
eye. No [buff](status-effects.md#buffs-and-cooldowns) is shared, and a companion she keeps lends
her nothing.

## Two kept beasts beside you

If a companion and a familiar are both beside you, and one of them has something to treat, the
[Heal](skills.md#skill-heal) ability first asks **which way**: a direction, or the wait key for
yourself. With only one beside you it asks nothing.
