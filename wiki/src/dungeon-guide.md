# The dungeons one by one

Most dungeons follow the rules on [Dungeons and danger](dungeons.md). The ones here have something
of their own: Grundlēas never ends, Fennmynster and Fyrdelf hide their ways in until a quest names
them, Ūtgang is found only from below, Ælfhām lies in the fey's own country, Dēopgang ends in a
town, and the four lairs each hold a criminal the Gilde wants dead.

## Grundlēas

**Grundlēas**, the groundless, has no last floor, and it forgets. Anyone may walk there and go
down. Every staircase, up or down, leads to a floor that has never been there before. Whatever you
left on the floor behind you is gone for good: every creature, [pile](objects.md#piles), and
[chest](chests.md), and your memory of it too. Going down and straight back up brings you to a
different floor, standing on a staircase down. Twenty floors down is twenty new floors back up. Up
from the first floor you come out on the overworld.

{{#include generated/dungeon-endless.md}}

Each floor's [danger](dungeons.md#who-lives-there-and-how-many) is its depth. Nothing lives here of
its own: every draw is a [stray](dungeons.md#who-lives-there-and-how-many), so anything that may
stray in the Ascmearc comes at its danger. No creature stands higher than the top of that roster,
so from the floor in the table on, the draws stay as they are while the floors go on.
[Items](magic-items.md) stop improving where the [item level](magic-items.md#finding-them) ends. The
[locks](doors.md#locked-doors) and the hidden things get harder with every floor down, without end.

Its [traps](traps.md) are [arrows](traps.md#trap-arrow), [spears](traps.md#trap-spear),
[pits](traps.md#trap-pit), and [alarms](traps.md#trap-alarm). It is [cold](temperature.md) (6 °C),
and it has no [shops](shops.md).

A floor pays [experience](experience.md#discovery) for arriving only if it is deeper than any you
have reached here.

Your [allies](allies.md) come with you as anywhere else. Those too far away stay behind, and that
is the last of them. Your [companion](companions.md#companions) or
[familiar](familiars.md#familiars) is never left behind, wherever it stands. A
[named creature](creatures.md) on a floor you leave has not met its end, and may turn up again.

### The keepers of the layers

Every tenth floor of Grundlēas is kept by a [named creature](creatures.md) of its own, in its lair.
That floor has only one stair down, in the lair, and it opens only when the keeper dies, at your
hand or any other. The stairs up are where they always are, so you may always leave.

If you leave with the keeper alive, the next floor of that depth holds it and its lair again, as
often as you come. It never follows you up or down the stairs. Once it is dead it never comes back,
and that depth's floors are ordinary from then on. A tenth floor deeper than the last keeper is
ordinary too.

## Fennmynster

**Fennmynster**, the fen minster, is what is left of a minster that sank into the marsh of
Micelfenn: eight floors of its cellars and crypts, at 6 °C. No road leads there, and its way in
cannot be found by looking. Sister Hild marks it on your map as you take up her
[quest](quests.md) **The fen minster**, the one after she has her chalice back.

The fen seeps into its rooms. One room in four is out of the ordinary (most floors have one or
two): [flooded](special-rooms.md#room-flooded) ankle-deep, slow and loud to wade,
[hushed](special-rooms.md#room-hushed), or [shadowed](special-rooms.md#room-shadowed). Its traps are
[pits](traps.md#trap-pit), [frost](traps.md#trap-frost), [alarms](traps.md#trap-alarm), and
[teleports](traps.md#trap-teleport). The monks hid their stores well: more of its
[doors are secret](doors.md#secret-doors) than in the other dungeons. No one trades down there;
Fennmynster has no [shops](shops.md).

Who lives there:

- **The marsh's beasts**: [leeches](creatures.md#creature-giant-leech),
  [frogs](creatures.md#creature-giant-frog), [crocodiles](creatures.md#creature-crocodile),
  [wisps](creatures.md#creature-will-o-wisp), [mud golems](creatures.md#creature-mud-golem), and
  [trolls](creatures.md#creature-troll), with the lesser water elementals the rain and the fen water
  make.
- **The minster's dead**: the [drowned](creatures.md#creature-drowned), the peat-dark
  [bog bodies](creatures.md#creature-bog-body), and [ghasts](creatures.md#creature-ghast) and
  [fen ghouls](creatures.md#creature-fen-ghoul), whose claws hold fast.
  [Pale drinkers](creatures.md#creature-pale-drinker) and [mummies](creatures.md#creature-mummy) keep
  its crypts.
- **Restless spirits** that pass through its walls:
  [corpse candles](creatures.md#creature-corpse-candle),
  [poltergeists](creatures.md#creature-poltergeist), [ghosts](creatures.md#creature-ghost), the
  [shadows](creatures.md#creature-shadow) that drink your strength, and the
  [wraiths](creatures.md#creature-wraith) that drink your life. [Stone haunts](creatures.md#creature-stone-haunt)
  drift through the minster's walls.
- **The cult** that came to the ruin: [cultists](creatures.md#creature-cultist) and their priests.
  Somewhere in the dark is the [bog hag](creatures.md#creature-bog-hag), whom you meet once in your
  life.
- **Fey of the damp** haunt the cellars: [pixies](creatures.md#creature-pixie),
  [sprites](creatures.md#creature-sprite), [boggles](creatures.md#creature-boggle),
  [quicklings](creatures.md#creature-quickling), [satyrs](creatures.md#creature-satyr), and
  [darklings](creatures.md#creature-darkling).
- **The cold water** stirs with water and [frost motes](creatures.md#creature-frost-mote) and
  [lesser ice elementals](creatures.md#creature-lesser-ice-elemental).

At the very bottom lies the **sunken crypt**: a nave of
[shallow water](terrain.md#terrain-shallow-water) between the monks' tombs, where two of the
[drowned](creatures.md#creature-drowned) still stand guard. At its far end a dry dais holds an
altar with the minster reliquary on it, which Sister Hild's last quest asks for. The crypt is on
the eighth floor and nowhere else.

## Fyrdelf

**Fyrdelf**, the fire delving, is the [duergar](races.md#race-duergar)'s mine under the hills of
Ascdūn, below the volcano. No road leads there, and its way in cannot be found by looking. Factor
Wulfrun marks it on your map as you take up her [quest](quests.md) **The lost shaft**. She offers
it once you have been four floors down into Cealdhlāw for the reeve, and gives it to you only once
you have reached level 5.

Its first four floors are the galleries: rooms and passages at 10 °C. The eight below are the deep
workings: wide caves that grow hotter toward the volcano's heart, 16 °C, then 20 °C, and 24 °C at
the bottom. Its traps are [arrows](traps.md#trap-arrow), [spears](traps.md#trap-spear),
[fireballs](traps.md#trap-fireball), [lightning](traps.md#trap-lightning-bolt), and
[alarms](traps.md#trap-alarm). Few of its [doors are hidden](doors.md#secret-doors), and its locks
are the hardest to pick of the three dungeons.

Who lives there:

- **The [duergar](races.md#race-duergar)** dig here: [miners](creatures.md#creature-duergar-miner),
  [warriors](creatures.md#creature-duergar-warrior), and [smiths](creatures.md#creature-duergar-smith).
- **Creatures of fire and rock**: [ember beetles](creatures.md#creature-ember-beetle),
  [salamanders](creatures.md#creature-fire-salamander),
  [magma crawlers](creatures.md#creature-magma-crawler),
  [lesser fire elementals](creatures.md#creature-lesser-fire-elemental),
  [cinder elementals](creatures.md#creature-cinder-elemental),
  [earth elementals](creatures.md#creature-earth-elemental) that move through the rock,
  [ash wraiths](creatures.md#creature-ash-wraith), and [basilisks](creatures.md#creature-basilisk).
- **Down from the hills above**: [ogres](creatures.md#creature-ogre), [trolls](creatures.md#creature-troll),
  and [hill trolls](creatures.md#creature-hill-troll).
- **The dead the delf's fires left**: [ogre zombies](creatures.md#creature-ogre-zombie) and
  [grave hounds](creatures.md#creature-grave-hound), [burning skulls](creatures.md#creature-burning-skull),
  the [charred dead](creatures.md#creature-charred-dead), [duergar](races.md#race-duergar) skeletons
  still in their helms, [rotting trolls](creatures.md#creature-rotting-troll), and the spirits of
  the miners that pass through the rock, [knockers](creatures.md#creature-knocker) and
  [spectres](creatures.md#creature-spectre).
- **The deep workings' dead** are many: [draugr](creatures.md#creature-draugr),
  [barghests](creatures.md#creature-barghest), [hands of glory](creatures.md#creature-hand-of-glory),
  vampire spawn, and the [gallows dead](creatures.md#creature-gallows-dead).
  [Hell hounds](creatures.md#creature-hell-hound), fiends at home in the fires, run the deep
  workings too.
- **The upper galleries** belong mostly to [kobolds](creatures.md#creature-kobold),
  [centipedes](creatures.md#creature-giant-centipede), and bats.
- **[Hobgoblin](creatures.md#creature-hobgoblin) war bands** come down to raid the
  [duergar](races.md#race-duergar): [soldiers](creatures.md#creature-hobgoblin),
  [archers](creatures.md#creature-hobgoblin-archer),
  [legionnaires](creatures.md#creature-hobgoblin-legionnaire) with spear and shield,
  [warcasters](creatures.md#creature-hobgoblin-warcaster), and
  [sergeants](creatures.md#creature-hobgoblin-sergeant).
- **Orc war bands** raid the mine too: [raiders](creatures.md#creature-orc-raider),
  [shamans](creatures.md#creature-orc-shaman), [berserkers](creatures.md#creature-orc-berserker),
  [veterans](creatures.md#creature-orc-veteran), [bowmen](creatures.md#creature-orc-bowman), and
  their [wargs](creatures.md#creature-warg), with [goblin wizards](creatures.md#creature-goblin-wizard)
  who call up more of their kin. Their [captains](creatures.md#creature-hobgoblin-captain) and
  [war chiefs](creatures.md#creature-orc-war-chief), like the duergar's
  [priests](creatures.md#creature-duergar-priest), live deeper than the delf goes.
- **Motes and elementals**: the motes of stone, fire, and air drift through the upper galleries;
  [spark motes](creatures.md#creature-spark-mote), lesser earth, air, and lightning elementals, and
  [water elementals](creatures.md#creature-water-elemental) through the workings.
- **Others**: the demons' fires draw up [lemures](creatures.md#creature-lemure) and
  [imps](creatures.md#creature-imp), and [meenlocks](creatures.md#creature-meenlock) lurk in the
  dark.

Every gallery floor holds a **foreman's cabin**: a timber-floored lodging where a duergar miner
keeps watch. Behind a plank wall it keeps a spare [pick-axe](digging.md#digging), his lamp, and a
[brass key](doors.md#keys) to the delf's locked rooms.
[Duergar traders](creatures.md#creature-duergar-trader) keep [general stores](shops.md) in the
galleries, where a room is fit for one (about one gallery floor in four).

On the eleventh floor, in a small chamber off a cave passage, stands a **hunter's statue**: a
[worn statue](terrain.md#terrain-statue) of a hooded figure on a raised floor between two torches,
and nothing else. Coming beside it reads its plinth: *"Letters are cut deep into the plinth, in the
old tongue: LĪF ÆR DĒAÞE. Below, in a later hand: 'Life before death. So the hunters swore, to the
one who waited for them on the road; and the road gave them each a blade.'"*

At the very bottom lies the **drake's hoard**: a hall ringed with lava where the
[fire drake](creatures.md#creature-fire-drake) sleeps on its gold, with a rare treasure and a magic
one. The drake lives nowhere else. Near the hoard, a second stair climbs from the drake's floor
into **Ūtgang**, the way out.

## Ūtgang

**Ūtgang**, the way out, is the duergar's old air-shaft workings: three floors of hewn rooms and
passages, with no caves, cut from the drake's deep up through the volcano's flank. Its bottom
floor's stairs down lead to the bottom of [Fyrdelf](#fyrdelf), beside the drake's hoard. Its top
floor's stairs up lead out onto the overworld.

That way in is [hidden](overworld.md#hidden-locations), and no quest tells of it. You find it by
climbing out, and from then on it is on your map: a quick way back down to the drake. Its floors
are as dangerous as the deep workings just above Fyrdelf's bottom (see the tables on
[Dungeons and danger](dungeons.md)). They are warmer the deeper they go, 14 °C, then 18 °C, and
22 °C at the bottom, with Fyrdelf's traps and locks, the deep workings' folk, and no shops.

## Dēopgang

**Dēopgang**, the deep way, goes down from the foot of Grægweall in the south-west corner. It is
the deepest [danger](dungeons.md#who-lives-there-and-how-many) of the march, 14 on its first floor
to 24 on its eleventh. Floors 1 to 4 are the deep gnomes' old cut galleries, where deep-gnome
traders sometimes keep a [general store](shops.md); floors 5 to 11 are the wild deep, wide caves.
Duergar, drow, trolls, elementals of stone and water, and the dead of old cave-ins live there.
Secret doors and [chests](chests.md) are commoner than elsewhere, and its rock gives only silver
and gold for ore, so a [miner](mining.md) finds [gems](gems.md) more often. The eleventh floor's
stairs down lead to [Gimmwīc](ascmearc.md#gimmwīc), the deep gnomes' town.

## Ælfhām

**Ælfhām**, the elves' home, is the hall of the Unseelie court, the dark one of the
[fey courts](creatures.md#factions). It lies behind a fairy mound in the holly wood near Holtwīc.
Its way in is [hidden](overworld.md#hidden-locations): the mound looks like any other stretch of
the wood.

Behind it is not the inside of a hill but the fey's own country: three floors of twisting caves at
4 °C, with no sun or moon. A dim violet [light](light.md#what-light-is) comes from nowhere, and
everywhere is just bright enough to see by, dimly.

Its [danger](dungeons.md#who-lives-there-and-how-many) runs from 4 to 6, and only fey live there:
[sprites](creatures.md#creature-sprite) and [pixies](creatures.md#creature-pixie),
[quicklings](creatures.md#creature-quickling) on the last floor, and
[boggles](creatures.md#creature-boggle), [darklings](creatures.md#creature-darkling), and
[redcaps](creatures.md#creature-redcap) [out of depth](dungeons.md#who-lives-there-and-how-many). No
[stray](dungeons.md#who-lives-there-and-how-many) finds the way in, but now and then a fey of the
bright court wanders the halls; it leaves you alone and fights its hosts. Ælfhām is thinly peopled.
Its [traps](traps.md) are [pits](traps.md#trap-pit) and [alarms](traps.md#trap-alarm), and it has no
[shops](shops.md) and no [special rooms](special-rooms.md).

On its third floor there is always the **moss bower**: a ring of
[glowing fungus](terrain.md#terrain-fungus) with a bed of moss in its middle, kept by a
[quickling](creatures.md#creature-quickling) and a [boggle](creatures.md#creature-boggle) of the
dark court. On the first floor, somewhere far off, pipes are playing.

## The lairs of the wanted

Four more dungeons are the lairs of the criminals the [Weyfarers' Gilde](guild.md#the-wanted) wants
dead. Each is four floors deep, of danger 6 on the first floor and 9 on the fourth. Each is hidden
until Wat One-Eye names its criminal and marks it on your map; the other three stay hidden for the
rest of your life. No one trades in any of them. At the bottom of each, and nowhere else, the
criminal waits in a room of their own:

- **Wyrtholm**, the herb islet, is the caves under an islet out in Micelfenn (9 °C).
  **Mother Sallow** brews there among the fen's leeches, frogs, crocodiles, wisps, mud golems, and
  marsh trolls, with footpads in her pay. Its traps are pits and alarms. Her **still-room** at the
  bottom holds her pots, her jars of potions, and angelica root.
- **Wulfeshol**, the wolf's hole, is a cave under Deorcholt. **Black Wystan**'s footpads and their
  archers keep watch there, with goblin bowmen he hires, and bats, centipedes, and barrow spiders
  in the dark. Its traps are pits, arrows, and alarms. At the bottom is the **robbers' hoard**: the
  coin of the Fennhām road with a rare thing among it, two footpads at the door, and him.
- **Bānfeld**, the bone field, is the grave passages under a barrow field on Hlāwdūn (8 °C).
  **Brother Osrick** keeps the dead he raised there: skeletons, zombies, ghouls, the drowned,
  ghosts, and the worms and spiders that feed where the dead are. One room in four is shadowed or
  hushed. Its traps are arrows, spears, pits, and alarms, and rusty keys open its locked rooms. At
  the bottom is the **unhallowed chapel**: bones heaped along the walls, two zombies in the aisle,
  and Osrick at an altar he defiled.
- **Brocenburg**, the broken fort, is the cellars under the ruined tower in Holegnwudu.
  **Sergeant Herrick** sells his axe to the goblins there. With him are goblins with their archers
  and chiefs, footpads who ran with him, bats in the broken roof, and now and then an ogre he
  hired, far stronger than the floor. Its traps are arrows, spears, and alarms, and iron keys open
  its locked rooms. At the bottom is the **captain's hall**: the old table broken up, the captain
  he killed under a cairn in the corner, and Herrick in the captain's place, with two goblins at
  the door.
