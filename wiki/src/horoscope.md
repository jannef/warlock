# Horoscope

Everyone is born under a sign: the **animal** and the **element** of the year they were born. You
do not choose yours: the stars decide. When you make a character (see
[Your character](character.md)), right after you choose your race the game tells you when you
were born and under which sign, and what the stars gave you (*Born under the stars*). The summary
and the character screen (`@`) show it again, for example *Age 34 · born 29 Greenleaf 1410 · year
of the Water Basilisk*, and the character screen lists your sign's gifts by name on its *Stars*
line.

## The Great Race

When the world was young, the years had no names, and the Keeper of Days could not tell one from
the next. So she called every beast of legend to a race. It would run from the Ash Peaks in the
north, down across the Glass Waste, over the Wide River, and through the Mistwood to the Gate of
Dawn by the sea. The first twelve through the gate would each have a year, in the order they came.

The **Salamander** was the smallest racer of all and could not swim, so it asked the Hydra to
carry it across the river. The Hydra was the strongest swimmer there and agreed. Its heads were
busy arguing about the way, and none of them noticed the Salamander climb up the middle neck. As
the Hydra reached the gate, the Salamander leapt down and through, first. The **Hydra** came
second.

The **Manticore** ran the whole course without resting once, and every racer who saw it coming
left the path; it came third. The **Unicorn** crossed the river leaping from stone to stone without
wetting a hoof, and came fourth.

The **Wyvern** could have flown in first. But it saw that the Mistwood was so thick that the
racers on foot were lost in it, so it stopped and burned a path through for them, and came fifth.
Then came the **Roc**, the mightiest of fliers, who had soared so high that it lost sight of the
gate and had to come down to look for it. It never knew that the **Basilisk** had been coiled
around its talon since the Ash Peaks. As the Roc landed, the Basilisk slid off in front of it, and
so the Basilisk came sixth and the Roc seventh.

The **Griffin** landed on every hilltop along the way to make sure that no one was after its
gold, and came eighth. The **Sphinx** stopped every racer it met to set them a riddle. Those who
answered went on ahead, and the Sphinx lost the whole morning; it came ninth. The **Phoenix**
burned to ash in the noon heat of the Glass Waste, rose again from its ashes at dusk, and flew on
to come tenth. The **Kraken** could not leave the water, so it swam the long way round, down the
river and along the coast, and came in on the evening tide, eleventh.

Last, at midnight, came the **Barghest**, the black hound. It had been fast enough to win, but it
had run behind the others all the way, to see that no racer was left behind. The Keeper of Days
was so moved that she gave it the last year, and set it to guard the gate between one great cycle
and the next. It stands there still.

So that no year would be lonely, the five elements, **Wood**, **Fire**, **Earth**, **Metal**, and
**Water**, agreed to walk beside the animals, one each year, in turn. The year of the race itself
was the year of the Wood Salamander. It is Year 1 of the reckoning.

## The great cycle

- Every year has an animal and an element. Both change on the 1st of Frostmere, the first day of
  the year.
- The animals follow the order of the Great Race. After the Barghest comes the Salamander again,
  so an animal comes back every 12 years.
- The elements follow the order Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water, so an element comes back every 5
  years.
- Together, every animal meets every element exactly once in a **great cycle** of 60 years. The
  first great cycle began in Year 1, the year of the race; the latest ones began in 1381 and 1441.
  The year 1444 is a Metal Unicorn year.

Your sign is the sign of the year you were born in, so the day matters at the turn of the year:
someone born on 30 Deepwinter is a sign earlier than someone born the next day, on 1 Frostmere.

## What a sign does

Your animal gives you **two gifts and a flaw**, and your element **two gifts** (elements have no
flaws). They are [talents](talents.md) of their own tree, *Stars*: given at birth, never picked,
and never lost. The game tells you what they do in words ("you move and act a little faster");
the tables below give the exact rules. Some animals' and elements' gifts are still to come: the
tables list those already in the game.

## Lucky days

Five signs have a **lucky day**: one animal for each element, born in its year **and** on one day
of the month (any month). The day is the animal's place in the Great Race: the Salamander came
first, so the 1st; the Manticore third, the Phoenix tenth, the Kraken eleventh; and the Barghest,
who guards the gate between the cycles, the 30th, the last day of the month. Someone born on a
lucky day has a **title** after their name ("Brakka, the Iron Scourge") and **one gift more**,
an unfair one. The game never mentions lucky days until a character is born on one.

A short-lived race reaches fewer years at a given start date, so its chances differ: a human or a
half-orc is likelier to be born lucky than an elf, and only the long-lived can be born the Racer
at the default start.

## Your age and birthday

Your starting age depends on your race: a human starts between 16 and 35 years old, an elf
between 90 and 300 (see [Races](races.md) for every race). Your birthday can be any day of the
year, and fate picks both: choosing another gender or class does not change them. Your birth date
is counted back from the day the run starts: you are exactly that old on it. You get a year older on every birthday, and the character screen shows your age on the day
you look.
