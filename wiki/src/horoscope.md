# Horoscope

Everyone is born under a sign: the **animal** and the **element** of the year they were born. You
do not choose yours; the stars decide. When you make a character (see
[Your character](character.md)), the game tells you right after you choose your race when you were
born, under which sign, and what the stars gave you (*Born under the stars*).

The summary and the [character screen](the-screen.md#character) (`@`) show it again, for example
*Age 34 · born 29 Greenleaf 1410 · year of the Water Basilisk*. The character screen also lists your
sign's gifts by name on its *Stars* line.

## The Great Race

When the world was young, the years had no names, and the Keeper of Days could not tell one from
the next. So she called every beast of legend to a race. It would run from the Ash Peaks in the
north, down across the Glass Waste, over the Wide River, and through the Mistwood to the Gate of
Dawn by the sea. The first twelve through the gate would each have a year, in the order they came.

The **Salamander** was the smallest racer of all and could not swim, so it asked the Hydra to
carry it across the river. The Hydra was the strongest swimmer there and agreed. Its heads were
busy arguing about the way, and none of them noticed the Salamander climb up the middle neck. As
the Hydra reached the gate, the Salamander leapt down and through, first. The **Hydra** came
second.

The **Manticore** ran the whole course without resting once, and every racer who saw it coming
left the path; it came third. The **Unicorn** crossed the river leaping from stone to stone without
wetting a hoof, and came fourth.

The **Wyvern** could have flown in first. But it saw that the Mistwood was so thick that the
racers on foot were lost in it, so it stopped and burned a path through for them, and came fifth.
Then came the **Roc**, the mightiest of fliers, who had soared so high that it lost sight of the
gate and had to come down to look for it. It never knew that the **Basilisk** had been coiled
around its talon since the Ash Peaks. As the Roc landed, the Basilisk slid off in front of it, and
so the Basilisk came sixth and the Roc seventh.

The **Griffin** landed on every hilltop along the way to make sure that no one was after its
gold, and came eighth. The **Sphinx** stopped every racer it met to set them a riddle. Those who
answered went on ahead, and the Sphinx lost the whole morning; it came ninth. The **Phoenix**
burned to ash in the noon heat of the Glass Waste, rose again from its ashes at dusk, and flew on
to come tenth. The **Kraken** could not leave the water, so it swam the long way round, down the
river and along the coast, and came in on the evening tide, eleventh.

Last, at midnight, came the **Barghest**, the black hound. It had been fast enough to win, but it
had run behind the others all the way, to see that no racer was left behind. The Keeper of Days
was so moved that she gave it the last year, and set it to guard the gate between one great cycle
and the next. It stands there still.

So that no year would be lonely, the five elements, **Wood**, **Fire**, **Earth**, **Metal**, and
**Water**, agreed to walk beside the animals, one each year, in turn. The year of the race itself
was the year of the Wood Salamander. It is Year 1 of the reckoning.

## The great cycle

- Every year has an animal and an element. Both change on the 1st of [Frostmere](time.md#the-calendar), the first day of
  the year.
- The animals follow the order of the Great Race. After the Barghest comes the Salamander again,
  so an animal comes back every 12 years.
- The elements follow the order Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water, so an element comes back every 5
  years.
- Together, every animal meets every element exactly once in a **great cycle** of 60 years. The
  first great cycle began in Year 1, the year of the race; the latest ones began in 1381 and 1441.
  The year 1444 is a Metal Unicorn year.

Your sign is the sign of the year you were born in, so the day matters at the turn of the year.
Someone born on 30 [Deepwinter](time.md#the-calendar) is a sign earlier than someone born the next
day, on 1 [Frostmere](time.md#the-calendar).

## What a sign does

Your animal gives you **two gifts and a flaw**. Your element gives you **two gifts**; elements have
no flaws. These are [talents](talents.md) of their own tree, *Stars*: given at birth, never picked,
and never lost. The game tells you what they do in words ("you move and act a little faster"); the
tables below give the exact rules. Some animals' gifts are still to come, and the tables list only
those already in the game.

Some gifts are things you do rather than rules that hold. You use them from the
[abilities menu](abilities.md#the-abilities-menu) (`a`), under *Birth sign*:

- Fire's *[Blaze](abilities.md#ability-blaze)*. Afterwards you are
  *[Burnt out](status-effects.md#status-burnt-out)* for a while, and cannot use it again until that
  passes.
- The Unicorn's **[Horn's grace](abilities.md#ability-horns-grace)** and the Basilisk's
  **[Transfixing stare](abilities.md#ability-transfixing-stare)**. Each then rests for a while (a
  [status](status-effects.md#how-they-work) that wears off) before you can use it again.

The [abilities](abilities.md) table lists them with everything else you can use.

Your animal also nudges where your [alignment](alignment.md#where-you-start) starts. The *Alignment*
column is what it adds to your starting score; elements add nothing. The game never shows the
number, only the band you start in.

## Picks for your sign

Every animal and every element also has **one talent of the Stars tree that only those born under
it may pick**. You pick it at a [level-up](experience.md#levelling-up) or at character
creation, like any other [talent](talents.md#choices-and-prerequisites) whose prerequisites hold.
Most ask for a [level](experience.md#levels) as well; a few ask for a [stat](stats.md) or a
[weapon skill](weapon-skills.md). The Talents page and the level-up screen show only your own
sign's.

Most of these picks make one of your gifts stronger. The Hydra's
*[Another head](horoscope.md#talent-another-head)* heals even while you starve, and the Phoenix's
*[Second rising](horoscope.md#talent-second-rising)* rises from the ashes once more. Two are things
you do, used from the [abilities menu](abilities.md#the-abilities-menu) under *Birth sign*: the
Wyvern's **[Fire breath](abilities.md#ability-fire-breath)** and the Barghest's
**[Hound's bay](abilities.md#ability-hounds-bay)**. Each rests a while after use.

The Phoenix's *[Rise from the ashes](horoscope.md#talent-rise-from-ashes)* comes once in a life,
twice with the pick *[Second rising](horoscope.md#talent-second-rising)*. Once you have risen, the
gift is spent (the [character screen](the-screen.md#character) says so), and the next death is your
last.

*[Hound's nose](horoscope.md#talent-hounds-nose)* tells you in a word on the eat list how fresh
[food](food.md#food-spoils) is: fresh, turning, or rotten. Food that is turning is still fine to
eat; rotten food is not.

*[Gatekeeper](horoscope.md#talent-gatekeeper)* strikes a foe that steps up beside you only sometimes,
depending on your [stance](stances.md#stances) (see [Free blows](stances.md#free-blows)):

| Stance | Chance |
|---|---|
| [Frenzy](stances.md#stance-frenzy) | always |
| [Aggressive](stances.md#stance-aggressive) | 1 in 2 |
| [Neutral](stances.md#stance-neutral) | 1 in 4 |
| [Defensive](stances.md#stance-defensive) | 1 in 8 |
| [Coward](stances.md#stance-coward) | 1 in 16 |

Your animal and your element also each change your starting [stats](stats.md) a little, and both
count. The changes are in the tables at the end of this page. They are added to your race's,
gender's, and class's (see [Your character](character.md)). The changes are small and fixed: the
horoscope rolls no dice.

## Lucky days

Five signs have a **lucky day**: one animal for each element, born in its year **and** on one day
of the month (any month). The day is the animal's place in the Great Race. The Salamander came
first, so its day is the 1st; the Manticore's is the 3rd, the Phoenix's the 10th, the Kraken's the
11th. The Barghest, who guards the gate between the cycles, has the 30th, the last day of the month.

Someone born on a lucky day has a **title** after their name ("Brakka, the Iron Scourge") and
**one gift more**, an unfair one. The game never mentions lucky days until a character is born on
one.

A short-lived race reaches fewer years at a given start date, so its chances differ. A
[human](races.md#race-human) or a [half-orc](races.md#race-half-orc) is likelier to be born lucky
than an [elf](races.md#race-elf), and only the long-lived can be born the Racer at the default
start.

## Your birthday, your month, and the full moon

The [calendar](time.md#the-calendar) and the moons touch a few gifts, a little:

- On your birthday you [heal](healing.md#how-fast-you-heal) faster.
- In the month you were born, your sign's actives rest sooner.
- While the [moon](sun.md#moonlight) is full and up, the gifts that reach out reach farther.

## Your age and birthday

Your starting age depends on your race. A [human](races.md#race-human) starts between 16 and 35
years old, an [elf](races.md#race-elf) between 90 and 300 (see [Races](races.md) for every race).
Your birthday can be any day of the year.

Your birth date is counted back from the day the run starts, so you are exactly that old on it. You
get a year older on every birthday, and the [character screen](the-screen.md#character) shows your
age on the day you look.
