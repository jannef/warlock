# Your lantern

A lantern is a [tool](objects.md#class-tools). It gives light only while it is ready in the
**[Tool](equipment.md#what-fits-where)** slot of your [equipment](equipment.md), where a
[pick-axe](digging.md#digging) would go, so you hold one or the other. It weighs 400 g. Every
[race](races.md) without [darkvision](vision.md#the-four-types) starts with one ready, unlit:
[elves](races.md#race-elf), [half-elves](races.md#race-half-elf), [humans](races.md#race-human),
[halflings](races.md#race-halfling), [gnomes](races.md#race-gnome),
[lizardfolk](races.md#race-lizardfolk), and [ratfolk](races.md#race-ratfolk). The others see in the
dark and start without one.

Press `u` (use a tool) to change what it does: from unlit to lit, from lit to hooded, and from
hooded back to unlit. Lighting it or putting it out is a [normal action](turns.md#what-actions-cost)
(6 seconds). Lowering the [hood](lantern.md#the-hood) takes half that (3 seconds). Its name says how
it is: "a lantern (lit)", "a lantern (hooded)". A lantern you take off or drop stays as it was, but
gives no light until it is ready in the Tool slot again.

A lit lantern is a [light source](light.md) that moves with you: a warm light whose reach is given
in the [table of lights](light.md#light-sources). It burns calmly; it
[flickers](light.md#what-is-only-for-show) a little, but less than a torch. What it lights, your
[vision](vision.md) can see. It is the answer to dark [dungeons](dungeons.md), and to nights
outdoors when the [moon](sun.md#moonlight) gives little or no light, or is hidden by
[clouds](weather.md#clouds-and-light). In [daylight](sun.md#the-suns-path) you do not need it.

Light works both ways, though. [Creatures](creatures.md) see by the same light you do. A lit
lantern shows you to every creature that has a clear line to you and is near enough, even one whose
eyes need light. With the lantern out, standing in the dark hides you from such eyes, and you can
watch creatures that carry their own light without being seen. See [Creatures](creatures.md).

## The hood

Your lantern has a hood. `u` on a lit lantern lowers it, and `u` again puts the lantern out. Lit
again, it burns open.

Hooded, the lantern gives half its light and reaches 2 cells instead of 5 (see the [table of
lights](light.md#light-sources)). The hood also keeps the light off you: it lights the ground
around you, but not the cell you stand on.

- **You see less.** With [normal eyes](vision.md#the-four-types) you see the 8 cells around you by
  it, and no farther. They get about 24 % of full light straight beside you and 19 % on the
  diagonals, and normal eyes need 16 %. [Low-light eyes](vision.md#the-four-types) see out to 2
  cells, except the far corners. [Darkvision](vision.md#the-four-types) is as good as ever.
- **You are seen less.** Your own cell gets none of the lantern's light, only a glimmer reflected
  off the ground nearby: at most an eighth of the light around you, too little for any eyes. So
  creatures whose eyes need light do not see you by it, even right next to you, while you see them
  in its ring. Creatures with [darkvision](vision.md#the-four-types) still see you within their
  darkvision, and any other light that falls on you (a torch, daylight) shows you as usual.

There is no fuel yet: the lantern burns as long as you leave it lit. The
**[Lamplighter](talents-exploring.md#talent-lamplighter)** [talent](talents.md) makes it burn brighter and
reach a cell farther, so you are also seen from a little farther away. Hooded, it gives the same
hooded light as before.

With no light ready, `u` asks which way to dig if you have a [digging](digging.md#digging) tool
ready. Otherwise it tells you that you have none, which takes no time.

See also: [Equipment](equipment.md), [Sight and memory](sight.md), and [Sun and moon](sun.md).
