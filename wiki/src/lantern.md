# Your lantern

You carry a lantern. Press `*` to light it, and `*` again to douse it. Each is a normal action (6
seconds), and the lantern starts unlit.

A lit lantern is a [light source](light.md) that moves with you: a warm light whose reach is in
the [table of lights](light.md#light-sources). It burns calmly: it flickers a little, but less
than a torch. What it lights is what your [vision](vision.md) can see, so it is the answer to dark
dungeons and to night outdoors, where the moon gives little light or none, or is hidden by
clouds. In daylight you do not need it.

Light works both ways, though. Creatures see by the same light you do, so a lit lantern shows you
to every creature with a clear line to you that is near enough to see you, even one whose eyes
need light. With the lantern out, standing in the dark hides you from such eyes, and you can
watch creatures that carry their own light without being seen. See [Creatures](creatures.md).

## The hood

Your lantern has a hood. [Hood your lantern](abilities.md) in the abilities menu (`a`) lowers it,
and again raises it. Lowering or raising the hood takes half a normal action (3 seconds); on a
lantern that is out, hooding it lights it with the hood lowered, which takes the time of both (9
seconds). Dousing a hooded lantern raises the hood: lit again, it burns open. The status bar says
`Lantern hooded` while it is.

Hooded, the lantern gives a third of its light and reaches 2 cells instead of 5 (see the [table of
lights](light.md#light-sources)), and the hood keeps the light off you: it lights the ground
around you but not the cell you stand on.

- **You see less.** With normal eyes you see the 8 cells around you by it and no farther (they get
  about 24 % of full light straight beside you, 19 % on the diagonals, and normal eyes need 16 %).
  Low-light eyes see out to 2 cells, but for the far corners. Darkvision is as good as ever.
- **You are seen less.** Your own cell gets none of the lantern's light, only a glimmer of it
  reflected off the ground nearby (at most an eighth of the light around you, too little for any
  eyes), so creatures whose eyes need light do not see you by it, even next to you, while you see
  them in its ring. Creatures with darkvision still see you within their darkvision, and any light
  that falls on you (a torch, daylight) shows you as usual.

There is no fuel yet: the lantern burns as long as you leave it lit. The **Lamplighter**
[talent](talents.md) makes it burn brighter, a cell farther (and so you are seen from a little
farther too); hooded, it gives the hooded light as before.

If a run has no lantern, `*` tells you so and takes no time. The lantern is part of how a scenario
starts, and every scenario in the current game gives you one.
