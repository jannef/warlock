# Animated objects

Things a will set moving and left behind: a candlestick that rocks after you, a teapot that
scalds, a broom that scuttles, a rug that wraps around your legs. They set on the living who
come near and leave everything else alone. The household ones are almost harmless, and worth
killing for what they are made of: a silver candlestick is a prize at any level. Deeper, a
sword, an axe, or a staff dances alone: an **[animated blade](creatures.md#creature-animated-blade-5)**, quick and fragile, and its weapon
the prize. Deeper still walk the composites: **[animated armour](creatures.md#creature-animated-armour-12)**, a suit with nobody inside that
fights with its gauntlets, and the **[animated panoply](creatures.md#creature-animated-panoply-20)**, the full kit of a knight with no knight,
[wielding and wearing](equipment.md#what-fits-where) real [magic items](magic-items.md) whose powers are its own. A panoply is among the most
dangerous things in the game, and among the most rewarding.

## What they are made of

An animated household thing *is* a real household object: an animated candlestick is a
candlestick, of brass, iron, pewter, or (rarely) silver. Its colour on the map is its
material's, and [look mode](controls.md#look-mode) names it: "an animated candlestick of
silver (hostile, unhurt), rocking". A [clatter of cutlery](creatures.md#creature-animated-cutlery) is three to six pieces of cutlery at
once.

An animated blade is one real melee weapon, of a kind found at its level or below, which it
**wields** as anyone would: the weapon's [damage](combat.md#damage), [threat](combat.md#criticals), and any [magic](magic-items.md) on it (a flaming blade
burns), with the blade's [Strength](stats.md#stat-strength) and a [rank](weapon-skills.md#marks-and-ranks) in the weapon's skill that grows with its level.
The deeper it is met, the likelier its weapon is magic, and from level 15 now and then [rare](magic-items.md#rarity). It
shows on the map as its weapon does, `)` in the colour of the weapon's material, and look mode
names what it wields as it would anyone's: "an animated blade (hostile, unhurt), wielding a
steel long sword". Like any weapon a monster carries, it is [unidentified](magic-items.md#unidentified-items) until you hold it. A
blade is hard to hit (its thin edge), shrugs off thrusts ([piercing](damage-types.md#damage-piercing)), and breaks under heavy
blows ([bludgeoning](damage-types.md#damage-bludgeoning)); it floats without a sound.

Animated armour and a panoply are made of several real things at once: body armour always, a
helm, a shield, gauntlets, and for a panoply a melee weapon and, the deeper it is, boots, a cloak,
an amulet, and a ring on each hand (from level 50). Each piece is drawn as a blade's weapon is
and worn or wielded as anyone's: the armour's [Protection](combat.md#protection), the shield's [Defence](combat.md#defence), the weapon's
blows. A panoply that wields a two-handed weapon carries no shield. Both show on the map as `A`,
in the colour of their body armour's material; look mode names what they wear and wield: "an
animated armour (hostile, unhurt), wearing a bronze cap and a padded coat". Animated armour has no
weapon: it slams with its gauntlets. Both are [slow](turns.md#speed) and [loud](hearing.md#how-loud), [open doors](doors.md#creatures-and-doors), and shrug off some of a
thrust (the armour, whose blows pass through the hollow). A panoply holds a [rank](weapon-skills.md#marks-and-ranks) in its weapon's
skill as a blade does, and beside a shield ranks in [Sword and Board](fighting-styles.md#style-sword-and-board).

All blades are one kind of creature in every way that counts: you learn of animated blades once,
a [quest](quests.md) to kill them counts every one, and they are drawn onto a floor as one kind, at the level
of the floor's [danger](dungeons.md#who-lives-there-and-how-many) (a blade of level 12 on a floor of danger 12, never below level 5 or above
20). So are all animated armours and all panoplies, each among themselves.

When one is destroyed, what it was made of falls **whole** half the time, as the object it was
(a silver candlestick you can [sell](shops.md#selling)). Otherwise it **breaks into the parts it was made of**: a
[gem](gems.md) set in it falls loose, every other part falls as [pieces](objects.md#pieces) of its material (a silver
candlestick as pieces of silver, still worth something), and paper and wax are lost. Whatever
magic it had is gone with it. They
leave no [corpse](food.md#corpses) and never [bleed](status-effects.md#bleeding).

## What they are

They are constructs: nothing lives in them to sicken, they have no blood or breath for poison,
and nothing in them bleeds, so they are never **[diseased](status-effects.md#status-diseased)**, **[poisoned](status-effects.md#status-poisoned)**, or **[bleeding](status-effects.md#bleeding)**. They
can still be [frightened](status-effects.md#status-frightened) (rarely: their will is not their own), [held](status-effects.md#status-held), [staggered](status-effects.md#status-staggered), or [transfixed](status-effects.md#status-transfixed).
What they are made of decides what hurts them most: wood and cloth [burn](damage-types.md#damage-fire), metal takes [lightning](damage-types.md#damage-electricity)
badly, and glass, stone, and bone shatter under heavy blows. Nothing can be [stolen](abilities.md#ability-steal) from them:
what they carry is what they are.

## What their magic does for them

Whatever an animated thing wears and wields works for it exactly as it would for you, rings and
amulets too: a Flaming sword [burns](damage-types.md#damage-fire), a ring of the Bear makes its blows harder, an amulet of the Ox
gives it more life, armour of the Salamander takes the edge off [fire](damage-types.md#damage-fire), a ring of Light makes it
[glow](light.md#what-light-is) (you see it coming). The gaps are few:

| Affix effect | Works for an animated thing? |

| [To hit](combat.md#hitting), [damage](combat.md#damage), added damage, [threat](combat.md#criticals), [criticals](combat.md#criticals), parrying | Yes |
| [Elemental damage](damage-types.md) (Flaming, Freezing, ...) | Yes |
| [Attack speed](turns.md#speed), [moving speed](turns.md#speed) | Yes |
| [Armour](combat.md#defence), shields, Dodge, Deflection, natural armour | Yes |
| [Protection](combat.md#protection) | Yes |
| [Resistances](damage-types.md#armour-and-resistance) | Yes |
| More [hit points](combat.md#hit-points) | Yes |
| [Regeneration](healing.md#how-fast) | Yes |

## Where they are found

The barrow of [Cealdhlāw](ascmearc.md#the-places) keeps candlesticks, cutlery, teapots, and cooking pots among its grave
goods; the drowned minster of [Fennmynster](ascmearc.md#the-places) its candlesticks, books, lanterns, and rugs. Blades
dance in the barrow's lower floors and all through [Fyrdelf](ascmearc.md#the-places), where the [duergar smiths'](creatures.md#creature-duergar-smith) work
wakes, and empty suits of armour walk Fyrdelf's deeper floors (they are of level 12 at least).
They never stray anywhere else.

Their homes are two [special rooms](special-rooms.md#homes-of-animated-things), which may turn
up in any [dungeon](dungeons.md) with a [danger](dungeons.md#who-lives-there-and-how-many), whatever lives there: an **[armoury](special-rooms.md#room-armoury)**, weapon racks along its
walls and blades, hollow armour, and panoplies asleep among them, drawn as if the level were
four [dangers](dungeons.md#who-lives-there-and-how-many) deeper; and a **[workshop](special-rooms.md#room-workshop)**, workbenches along its walls and the household things of
a house's work asleep about them. Fyrdelf favours [armouries](special-rooms.md#room-armoury), and on its bottom floor an
armoury is where a panoply stands; no dungeon of the [Ascmearc](ascmearc.md) goes deep enough for one to walk
its halls.

## In numbers

Every animated type is generated from its tier and its body by the formulas below (rounded down
part by part): [Strength](stats.md#stat-strength) 11 + 0.4 × level, [Toughness](stats.md#stat-toughness) 12 + 0.4 × level, [Dexterity](stats.md#stat-dexterity) 10 + 0.25 ×
level, [Willpower](stats.md#stat-willpower) 16 + 0.35 × level, [Intelligence](stats.md#stat-intelligence) and [Presence](stats.md#stat-presence) 2 (they are mindless), then the
tier's adjustments; [hit points](combat.md#hit-points) beyond Toughness 2.5 × level × the tier's factor; [damage](combat.md#damage) dice the
nearest of 1d2, 1d3, … 5d12 to 2 + 0.55 × level, moved by the tier's steps. A blade has no dice
of its own (it strikes with its weapon); its [speed](turns.md#speed) is 1200 + 5 × level (at most 1600) and its
natural [Defence](combat.md#defence) 2 + level / 10.

### Blades

A blade's weapon is drawn among the melee weapons found at its level or below, by how common
each is, then made **[at least magic](magic-items.md#rarity)** by the chance below (and a magic one may come out [rare](magic-items.md#rarity), as
any find may), or **[rare](magic-items.md#rarity)** outright by the second chance, at an [item level](magic-items.md#base-items) equal to the blade's;
otherwise it is plain, of a [material](objects.md#materials) drawn as a monster's gear is. Its [rank](weapon-skills.md#marks-and-ranks) in the weapon's skill
is 1 + level / 10, at most 8.

Animated armour and a panoply draw each of their pieces the same way, slot by slot in the order
below: each line is tried at its chance, from the level it names, as many times as it says (a
ring for each hand); a slot already taken (the off hand beside a two-handed weapon) is passed
over. Animated armour's gauntlets strike with the dice of its level; its [Strength](stats.md#stat-strength) is the role's.
A panoply's rank in [Sword and Board](fighting-styles.md#style-sword-and-board) beside a shield is level / 15.
