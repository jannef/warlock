# Thief

{{#include generated/class-levels.md:thief}}

The thief's [sneak attacks](#talent-sneak-attack) build
[openings](openings.md). [Exploit](abilities.md#ability-exploit), theirs from
level 1, spends every one they hold on a single sneak attack, deeper for each, and
[Low blow](#talent-low-blow) at 12, [Improved sneak attack](#talent-improved-sneak-attack)
at 32, and [Master thief](#talent-master-thief) at 60 add to it (see
[Exploit](openings.md#exploit)). From level 18 [Thrown exploit](openings.md#thrown-exploit)
makes the same blow with a thrown weapon, at a foe up to 6 cells away. The thief has no
[stamina](resources.md#stamina) until a [weapon rank](weapon-skills.md#raising-a-weapon-skill) teaches an active
ability.
