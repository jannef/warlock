# Stats

You have six **stats**, each a number that says how strong, quick, hardy, clever, steadfast, or
striking you are. They are inspired by the abilities of Dungeons & Dragons, and three of them go
by other names here: Toughness is D&D's Constitution, Willpower its Wisdom, and Presence its
Charisma.

{{#include generated/stats.md}}

Each scenario sets your stats when a run starts; there is no character creation yet.

## Seeing your stats

Press `@` to open the **character screen**. It shows your name, your stats in the order above
(with their short names), and your weight: your own, plus what you carry (see
[Objects and your pack](objects.md)). Looking takes no time; press `@` or `Esc` to close it.

Creatures have stats too, but you cannot see theirs.

## What they do

Nothing, yet. No rule uses stats so far: they do not change how far you see, how quietly you
walk, how much you can carry, or how fights go, and nothing raises or lowers them.
