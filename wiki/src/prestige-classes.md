# Prestige classes

A **prestige class** is a second table of levels for a class that stops short of the rest, as the
[bard](classes.md#bard) does at 30. You reach it only by a **calling**: a deed found somewhere in
the world, never by experience alone. The game never names one, but the world leaves hints, such
as a [statue](terrain.md#walls)'s inscription.

There is no multiclassing. You keep the class you chose for good, and the prestige class adds to
it.

- **Your level is your total.** A bard of level 30 called into a prestige class becomes Hunter 1
  at level 31, and Hunter 30 at 60. The [status bar](the-screen.md#the-screen) shows the total.
  The [character screen](character.md) shows both: `Bard 30, Hunter 4`.
- **The cap.** Each class has a last level (the [table](experience.md#levels) says which). Past it
  your [experience](experience.md) still counts, but buys no level. A calling raises your cap by
  the prestige class's levels.
- **The calling** is open to a character of a class it calls from, at the level it asks (that
  class's last), who has no prestige class yet. Walking through it calls you at once, and you are
  told so. If you have less experience than the next level costs, you are given exactly what you
  lack, so your first prestige level comes at once. Experience banked past your old cap buys
  every level it can, all at once.
- **Everything carries across.** Your class's abilities, talents, skills, hit points, and
  [mana](mana.md) stay as they were. Each level from then on is the prestige class's: its
  [hit points](hit-points.md#hit-points), its mana, its [skill rolls](skills.md#getting-better) and
  the skills it is known for, its bonus [talents](talents.md), and its abilities, at its own
  levels. What a level gives by your total still holds: no hit points past 50 and no mana past 60
  (see [What a level gives](experience.md#what-a-level-gives)). So does the experience your race
  and first class need.

The **Hunter** has sworn the old oath and been answered: a fighter whose weapon comes when called.

**[Spirit weapon](abilities.md#ability-spirit-weapon)**, from Hunter 1, calls a weapon of
spirit-stuff into your hand. It takes the shape of any melee weapon you choose, for the time of
wielding one and nothing else. It is a plain weapon of that kind in every way (its damage, weight,
and grip), with +3 [to hit](combat.md#hitting) and a [threat](combat.md#criticals) 2 lower, and it
ignores every point of the target's [Protection](combat.md#protection). It fades the moment it
leaves your hands, and cannot be thrown (see [Spirit weapon](abilities.md#ability-spirit-weapon)).
