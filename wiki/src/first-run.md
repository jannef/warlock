# Your first run

New to Warlock? Make a [warrior](class-warrior.md), walk into the little cave beside the pass
where you start, then on to the market town to the east. Rest when you are hurt, eat when you are
hungry, and leave a fight that is going badly. Death is final. This page tells you the rest of what
you need for your first hour; the other pages tell you everything else.

## Make a character

Choose **New run** on the main menu. You pick your [race](races.md), your
[gender](character.md#gender), your [class](classes.md), two [talents](talents.md) (or let
**Random** pick them), and a name (see [Your character](character.md)). Any race will do for a
first run. Your class matters more.

## Choosing a class

The [warrior](class-warrior.md), the [berserker](class-berserker.md), and the
[paladin](class-paladin.md) are the best first picks. They have the most hit points, they start
with good arms, and they fight by walking into the enemy. Casters and the thief are harder: they
have few hit points and need to keep their distance or stay unseen. The everyday folk are hardest
of all, and two of them are a joke.

The key stats are the ones the class rolls dice to raise. Hit points and mana are what the class
adds to yours. Each class's page says how to play it (see [Classes](classes.md)).

## The keys you need first

These are the default keys. `?` shows them in the game, as you have bound them (see
[Controls](controls.md)).

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| numpad or arrows | Move; move into an enemy to attack it |
| `s` or numpad `5` | Wait a turn, searching the cells around you |
| `,` | Pick up what lies on your cell |
| `i` / `I` | Your equipment (wear and wield) / your pack |
| `a` | The [abilities menu](abilities.md#the-abilities-menu): your spells and class abilities |
| `t` | Shoot or throw what is ready in your Missile weapon slot |
| `e` | Eat or drink |
| `b` | [Rest](resting.md) |
| `>` / `<` | Go down / up stairs; `>` also enters a place on the overworld map |
| `@` | Your [character](the-screen.md#character): stats, level, and experience |
| `?` | Every key |
| `Esc` | The pause menu: save and quit |

## What the status bar tells you

The line under the map (see [The screen](the-screen.md)) shows your hit points as `HP 18/24`.
When something is wrong, it says so in words: `Bleeding`, `Poisoned`, or `Diseased` for what ails
you, `Hungry` or worse for [hunger](hunger.md), and `Burdened` or worse when you carry too much
(see [Burden](burden.md)). Each of these is a reason to stop and deal with it.

## Your first hour

The run starts at eight in the morning on **Beorggeat**, the pass on the western edge of
[the Ascmearc](ascmearc.md). Snow closes it behind you.

- **Lytelscræf**, a little cave two cells from the pass, is the easiest dungeon there is: three
  short floors of rats, bats, and kobolds, with a chest at the bottom (see
  [The dungeons](ascmearc.md#the-dungeons)). On the [overworld](overworld.md) map, walk onto it and
  press `>`.
- **Cēaptūn**, the market town, lies six cells east along the road, under two hours' walk (see
  [Cēaptūn](ascmearc.md#cēaptūn)). It is safe. Its [shops](shops.md) sell food, arms, and armour;
  at the Mynster you can rest, and Sister Hild cures and mends for gold; at the Gildhalle,
  Wat One-Eye gives out work (see [Quests](quests.md)).
- **Cealdhlāw**, the cold barrow in the downs east of the town, is the next dungeon, and harder
  (see [Dungeons and danger](dungeons.md)).

Pick up what the dead leave behind: arms, armour, potions, and gold. Sell what you don't need in
town, and buy food before you run short.

## When you level up

Kills and finished quests give you [experience](experience.md). At each new level your hit points
grow, and a screen opens by itself for the choices you make: skill rolls at every level, and a
talent at every third (see [When you level up](experience.md#when-you-level-up)).

## Staying alive

- **Rest when you are hurt.** Press `b` once no enemy is about. You heal twice as fast resting
  (see [Healing](healing.md)).
- **Eat when you are hungry.** Press `e`. Carry food; the corpses of what you kill can be eaten
  too, but not all of them safely (see [Food and corpses](food.md)).
- **Bleeding and poison** wear off by themselves, but they hurt, and they slow or stop your
  healing while they last. Potions, the Heal skill, and prayer end them sooner (see
  [Curing bleeding, poison, and disease](healing.md#curing-bleeding-poison-and-disease)).
- **Carry a light.** Most races start with a [lantern](lantern.md); press `u` to light it in a dark
  dungeon. Dwarves and the other races that see in the dark start without one.
- **Don't fight burdened.** Too much in your pack makes you slow and clumsy (see
  [Burden](burden.md)). Drop what you don't need with `d`.
- **Leave a losing fight.** Walk away, drink a healing potion, or take the stairs. A foe right
  beside you that knows you are there follows you up or down the stairs (see
  [Stairs](terrain.md#stairs)), so get a step ahead first.
- **Death is final.** When you die, your character's save is deleted. Your save is your character:
  you can stop and go on later, but never go back (see [Saving](saving.md) and
  [Death](hit-points.md#death)).
