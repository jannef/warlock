# Ki

The [ascetic](class-ascetic.md) gets ki, full, with
[martial arts](class-ascetic.md#talent-martial-arts) at level 1. The pool holds 4, plus your
[Willpower](stats.md#stat-willpower) [modifier](stats.md#modifiers), plus a point per 6
[levels](experience.md#levels), and never less than 3. For example, Willpower 12 holds 5 at level
1, Willpower 14 holds 10 at level 25, and Willpower 16 holds 17 at level 60.
[Inner calm](talents.md#talent-inner-calm) adds 2.

Ki comes back a point every 3 base turns (18 seconds), resting too.
[Perfect self](class-ascetic.md#talent-perfect-self) (level 60) brings it back twice as fast, a point
every 9 seconds. What spends it:

- Each second blow of a [flurry](class-ascetic.md#talent-flurry) (level 1) spends a point. With none
  left, the attack is a single blow.
- [Step of the wind](abilities.md#ability-step-of-the-wind) (level 6) spends 1 and takes no time.
- [Stunning strike](class-ascetic.md#talent-stunning-strike) (level 12) spends 1.
- [Patient defence](abilities.md#ability-patient-defence) (level 18) spends 1 and takes a wait's
  time.
- [Stillness](abilities.md#ability-stillness) (level 25) spends 2 and takes no time.
- [Diamond soul](class-ascetic.md#talent-diamond-soul) (level 40) spends 1 by itself on each missile it
  catches.
- [Quivering palm](class-ascetic.md#talent-quivering-palm) (level 50) spends 5.

## The talent on it

**[Inner calm](talents.md#talent-inner-calm)** (Finesse; [ascetic](class-ascetic.md), level 18):
ki holds 2 more and comes back half again as fast, a point every 12 seconds instead of every 18. Inner calm changes nothing until something gives the [ascetic](class-ascetic.md) [ki](resources.md#resource-ki).
