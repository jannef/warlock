## How to play

You fight up close and take the blows. Your sword, shield, jerkin, and helm make you hard
to hurt, and only the berserker has more [hit points](hit-points.md#hit-points). Keep to one kind
of weapon: [Weapon drill](#talent-weapon-drill) makes every melee
[weapon skill](weapon-skills.md#raising-a-weapon-skill) rise a quarter faster with use.
[Charge](#talent-charge), in the [abilities menu](abilities.md#the-abilities-menu) (`a`), rushes you
at a foe you see and strikes it, for [stamina](stances.md#stamina). Fight in corridors and
doorways, so foes come at you one at a time, and [rest](resting.md) between fights.
