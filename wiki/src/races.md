# Races

Your race is the first choice of a new run (see [Your character](character.md)). It changes your
starting [stats](stats.md), gives you your eyes (the [vision type](vision.md) you start with),
sets how old you can be when you start, adds to your hit points (see
[Combat](combat.md#hit-points)), trains you in a skill or two (humans: a little more in one of
their class's; see [Skills](skills.md#who-trains-what)), and may give you a rank in a
[weapon skill](weapon-skills.md) or two, added to your class's.

{{#include generated/races.md}}

The changes are added to the average score of 10, together with your gender's, your class's, and
your [birth sign's](horoscope.md). Most are plain numbers; a few are dice, rolled when you start:
`1d2+1` is one or two, plus one (see [How your stats add up](character.md#how-your-stats-add-up)).
Your starting age is picked at random within the race's range.

Races are not balanced against each other. Humans start with no gifts and no flaws, and the
others trade one strength for one weakness, or for two.

Halflings, gnomes, goblins, kobolds, and ratfolk are small, and carry three quarters of what the
others do; dwarves and duergar are not slowed or hindered by a load until they strain under it.
See [Burden](burden.md).

Races need different amounts of food: elves, drow, goblins, and kobolds least, halflings (second
breakfast) most. See [Hunger](hunger.md).

## The evil races

Six races start on the evil side of [alignment](alignment.md): goblins, kobolds, lizardfolk, and
ratfolk Callous, drow and duergar Evil. Some are poor choices on purpose, and their levels come
quicker for it (see [Experience](experience.md)).

- **Goblins** are the quick, crappy choice: weak and frail but quick-handed, sneaky (Stealth),
  short-lived, and light eaters, and their levels come fastest but for the kobolds'.
- **Drow**, the elves of the deep dark, are graceful and sharp but frail, a little resistant to
  poison, and notice every hidden thing in passing as elves do. Strong light dazzles them (below).
  Long-lived, their levels come slowly.
- **Kobolds** are the weakest of all, but good with traps: they notice hidden traps in passing
  (never a secret door), find hidden things more easily, and start trained in Disable device. With
  their huge families, their levels come cheapest of all.
- **Lizardfolk** are strong and tough, dull and alien, with scales that soak a point of every
  physical blow (natural armour, as a creature's hide: see [Combat](combat.md#protection)).
  Cold-blooded, they will be slower in the cold once the cold slows anyone.
- **Duergar**, the dwarves' grey cousins, are tough and dour; like dwarves they shrug off a load
  until they strain under it and notice what is hidden in stone.
- **Ratfolk** are nimble scavengers: weak and shunned, but hard to sicken (a bonus on every check
  against a [disease](diseases.md)), and they eat rotten food without harm (see
  [Food](food.md)).

No creature takes your side for your race: hostile kobolds attack a kobold too. Good folk greet
an Evil character coldly, but that is your alignment, not your race.

**Strong light** dazzles drow: while the light on your own cell is bright enough (the sun well
up under a clear sky, a bright fire close by; their own lantern alone is not), you see nothing
farther than ten cells and fight a little worse. The game tells you when it begins and ends,
and the status bar shows `Dazzled` while it lasts.
