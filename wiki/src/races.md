# Races

Your race is the first choice of a new run (see [Your character](character.md)). It changes your
starting [stats](stats.md), gives you your eyes (the [vision type](vision.md#the-four-types) you start with),
sets how old you can be when you start, adds to your [hit points](combat.md#hit-points) (see
[Combat](combat.md#hit-points)), trains you in a [skill](skills.md) or two ([humans](races.md#race-human): a little more in one of
their class's; see [Skills](skills.md#who-trains-what)), may give you a rank in a
[weapon skill](weapon-skills.md) or two, added to your class's, and adds a few things to your
[starting kit](starting-kit.md): an [elf](races.md#race-elf)'s waybread, a [dwarf](races.md#race-dwarf)'s pick-axe, a [halfling](races.md#race-halfling)'s pipe.

{{#include generated/races.md}}

The changes are added to the average score of 10, together with your gender's, your [class](classes.md)'s, and
your [birth sign's](horoscope.md#what-a-sign-does). Most are plain numbers; a few are dice, rolled when you start:
`1d2+1` is one or two, plus one (see [How your stats add up](character.md#how-your-stats-add-up)).
Your starting age is picked at random within the race's range.

Races are not balanced against each other. [Humans](races.md#race-human) start with no gifts and no flaws, and the
others trade one strength for one weakness, or for two.

[Halflings](races.md#race-halfling), [gnomes](races.md#race-gnome), [goblins](races.md#race-goblin), [kobolds](races.md#race-kobold), and [ratfolk](races.md#race-ratfolk) are small, and carry three quarters of what the
others do; [dwarves](races.md#race-dwarf) and [duergar](races.md#race-duergar) are not slowed or hindered by a load until they strain under it.
See [Burden](burden.md).

Races need different amounts of [food](food.md): [elves](races.md#race-elf), [drow](races.md#race-drow), [goblins](races.md#race-goblin), and [kobolds](races.md#race-kobold) least, [halflings](races.md#race-halfling) (second
breakfast) most. See [Hunger](hunger.md).

## The evil races

Six races start on the evil side of [alignment](alignment.md): [goblins](races.md#race-goblin), [kobolds](races.md#race-kobold), [lizardfolk](races.md#race-lizardfolk), and
[ratfolk](races.md#race-ratfolk) Callous, [drow](races.md#race-drow) and [duergar](races.md#race-duergar) Evil. Some are poor choices on purpose, and their [levels](experience.md#levels) come
quicker for it (see [Experience](experience.md)).

- **[Goblins](races.md#race-goblin)** are the quick, crappy choice: weak and frail but quick-handed, sneaky ([Stealth](skills.md#skill-stealth)),
  short-lived, and light eaters, and their [levels](experience.md#levels) come fastest but for the kobolds'.
- **[Drow](races.md#race-drow)**, the [elves](races.md#race-elf) of the deep dark, are graceful and sharp but frail, a little [resistant](damage-types.md#armour-and-resistance) to
  [poison](damage-types.md#damage-poison), and [notice every hidden thing in passing](searching.md#noticing-in-passing) as elves do. Strong light dazzles them (below).
  Long-lived, their [levels](experience.md#levels) come slowly.
- **[Kobolds](races.md#race-kobold)** are the weakest of all, but good with [traps](traps.md#finding-traps): they [notice hidden traps in passing](searching.md#noticing-in-passing)
  (never a [secret door](doors.md#secret-doors)), find hidden things more easily, and start trained in [Disable device](skills.md#skill-disable-device). With
  their huge families, their [levels](experience.md#levels) come cheapest of all.
- **[Lizardfolk](races.md#race-lizardfolk)** are strong and tough, dull and alien, with scales that soak a point of every
  physical blow (natural armour, as a [creature](creatures.md)'s hide: see [Combat](combat.md#protection)).
  Cold-blooded, they will be slower in the [cold](temperature.md) once the cold slows anyone.
- **[Duergar](races.md#race-duergar)**, the [dwarves](races.md#race-dwarf)' grey cousins, are tough and dour; like dwarves they shrug off a [load](burden.md)
  until they strain under it and [notice what is hidden](searching.md#noticing-in-passing) in stone.
- **[Ratfolk](races.md#race-ratfolk)** are nimble scavengers: weak and shunned, but hard to sicken (a bonus on every [check](diseases.md#checks)
  against a [disease](diseases.md)), and they eat rotten food without harm (see
  [Food](food.md)).

No [creature](creatures.md) takes your side for your race: [hostile](creatures.md#factions) [kobolds](races.md#race-kobold) attack a kobold too. Good folk greet
an Evil character coldly, but that is your [alignment](alignment.md), not your race.

**Strong [light](light.md#what-light-is)** dazzles [drow](races.md#race-drow): while the light on your own cell is bright enough (the [sun](sun.md#sky-light) well
up under a clear sky, a bright fire close by; their own [lantern](lantern.md) alone is not), you see nothing
farther than ten cells and fight a little worse. The game tells you when it begins and ends,
and the [status bar](controls.md#the-screen) shows `Dazzled` while it lasts.
