# Quests

Some of the [people you meet](npcs.md) have work for you. Quests are simple errands, as in ADOM:
kill something, bring something, go somewhere, or carry something to someone else. Do it, come
back, and you are rewarded with gold, things, and [experience](experience.md).

## Getting a quest

Chat with someone (`C`). A quest they have for you is a topic on the dialogue screen, named after
the quest, listed before their other topics. Ask about it to hear what they want; the screen then
offers **Accept** and **Decline**. Declining costs nothing: ask again later and they offer it
again. An offer you have not answered stays open until you do (one per person at a time). Some
quests are only offered once you have done another one for the same person.

Talking about quests takes no time.

## Kinds of quests

- **Kill**: kill a number of creatures of one kind, sometimes in a particular place ("Kill 3
  giant rats in the Arena."). Only creatures you kill yourself count, with a weapon, a shot, or a
  throw; one killed by someone else, or by a trap, does not. Kills you made before you took the
  quest up count too, so the person is pleased to hear the job is done already.
- **Bring**: bring some things in your pack ("Bring 3 apples to the gardener."). They are taken
  from your pack when you hand the quest in; anything you carry beyond what was asked stays yours.
- **Reach**: get to a place, such as a level of a dungeon ("Reach the Depths 3."). Having been
  there before counts.
- **Deliver**: the person gives you something as you accept (you need room for it in your pack),
  to carry to someone else and hand in there. Without it in your pack you cannot hand it in: if
  you drop it, pick it up again.

When something you do brings a quest along, the message line says so: "Quest updated: 2 / 3
giant rats.", and when it is done, "Quest updated: 3 / 3 giant rats. Return to the old hermit."

## Handing in

Go back to whoever the quest is handed in to (the person who gave it, or for a delivery the one
you carry it to) and ask about the quest again. Before it is done they remind you of what they
asked; once it is, they take what they asked for, thank you, and pay: "Quest complete: Three
apples.", "The gardener gives you 10 gold coins.", "You gain 15 experience." What does not fit in
your pack is put at your feet. A few quests can be done again once they are done.

## The quest log

`q` opens the **quest log**: the quests you have taken up, then the ones you have done. Choose
one with the arrows to see what it asks, how far you have got ("2 / 3 killed.", "Return to the
old hermit."), and who gave it, in their words. It is page 5 of the info screens: `1` to `5`,
`Tab`, and `Shift`+`Tab` switch between it and the character screen, the inventory, the
equipment, and your skills.

To give a quest up, choose it and press `a`; the log asks first, and `Enter` abandons it (any
other key keeps it). The person who gave it will offer it again. Something they gave you to
deliver stays with you. Opening the log and abandoning take no time.
