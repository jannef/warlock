# Body talents

The [talent](talents.md) trees of the body: **Vigour** (hit points, healing, and constitution),
**Might** (Strength, blows, and breaking things), **Finesse** (Dexterity, dodging, and
quickness), and **Grit** (hunger, burden, and will). Each talent is listed with what it needs and
its rule, exactly (see [What a talent does](talents.md#what-a-talent-does)).
