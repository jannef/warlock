# Ammunition

Missile weapons shoot their own kind of ammunition: arrows for bows, bolts for crossbows, stones
for slings. Ammunition is stackable: a quiver of 24 arrows is one stack in your pack, readied in
the Missiles slot on the [equipment](equipment.md) screen. A shot takes one; where it comes down
it lies, to be picked up again and joined to the stack.

- **Breaking**: an arrow or a bolt that hits may break, by the chance in the table; one that
  misses never does. Stones and thrown weapons do not break.
- **Flaming arrows** never lie to be picked up: they burn where they land, lighting the dark
  around them, and then they are gone (see [Ranged combat](ranged.md)).
- Ammunition weighs what it weighs: a full quiver counts toward your weight like anything else
  ([Objects and your pack](objects.md)).
