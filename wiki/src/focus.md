# Focus

The [ranger](class-ranger.md) gets focus, full, with
[rapid shot](class-ranger.md#talent-rapid-shot) at level 12, and keeps it for good. A shot you loose
while you hold 2 focus spends them and takes 10 % less time. A [volley](class-ranger.md#talent-volley)
(level 50) costs 6. Focus comes back while you act, but not while you rest.

## The talent on it

**[Steady hand](talents.md#talent-steady-hand)** (Weaponcraft; [ranger](class-ranger.md), level
12): focus comes back half again as fast, a point every 4 seconds instead of every 6. It still
does not come back while you rest.
