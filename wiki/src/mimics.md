# Mimics

Not every [chest](chests.md) is a chest. Now and then what stands on the floor shaped as a
coffer, a hutch, or a strongbox is a **mimic**: a thing that sits in the dark looking like a
chest until something touches it, and grows bigger the longer it sits. It looks exactly like a
real chest of its kind, in sight and in memory, and [look mode](controls.md#look-mode) says the same words of it.

A [chest](chests.md) laid on a floor is a mimic by a small chance of its band, but never in a [sanctuary](special-rooms.md#room-sanctuary),
which creatures shun. A
mimic is always of the floor's level, as a [chest](chests.md) is, and is named by the same ten bands: a
**[mimicling](creatures.md#creature-mimic-1)** passes for a coffer, a **young mimic** for a chest, an **eldest mimic** for a
gilded ark. It carries exactly what the [chest](chests.md) would have held, and drops it all, whole, when it
dies: nothing in it is ever smashed or burnt.

## Finding one out

A mimic does nothing until it is found out: it never moves, never wakes on its own, and no
other creature takes any notice of it. It is found out when

- **you touch it**: walk into it, open it, [kick](doors.md#kicking) it, or [pick its lock](doors.md#picking-locks). It lurches ("The coffer
  lurches: it is a young mimic!") and strikes you at once, a free blow that may [hold](status-effects.md#status-held) you fast.
  Your action is not done and takes no time; the next move is yours.
- **you cast [Knock](abilities.md#ability-knock) on it**: it lurches, but no blow comes; the [mana](mana.md#how-much) is spent.
- **anything strikes it**: a blow, an arrow or a thrown dagger (which stop at it), a [fireball](magic.md#spell-fireball),
  a [trap's](traps.md#finding-traps) burst ("The coffer shudders: it is a young mimic!").
- **you find it by [searching](searching.md#searching-1)**: it is a hidden thing like a [secret door](doors.md#secret-doors), its difficulty
  12 + level ÷ 5 (and the branch's addition per depth); [in passing](searching.md#noticing-in-passing) only if you notice
  everything (trained in [Spot](skills.md#skill-spot), or an [elf](races.md#race-elf)). "You notice the coffer breathing: a young mimic!"

Until then it has seen no one, so a [thief's](classes.md#thief) [sneak attack](talents.md#talent-sneak-attack) lands on it. Once found out it is an
ordinary [hostile](creatures.md#factions) creature of the vermin and never disguises itself again. It is [slow](turns.md#speed) (speed
800): you can walk away from it.

## Talents

- **[Chest-wise](talents.md#talent-chest-wise)** (delver; [Intelligence](stats.md#stat-intelligence) 11 or [Detect traps](skills.md#skill-detect-traps) 15): within two cells
  you see a mimic for what it is: it shows as itself, not as a chest. It is still disguised: it
  waits, unaware, until something finds it out. And a mimic's grip never holds you.
