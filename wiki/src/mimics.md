# Mimics

Not every [chest](chests.md) is a chest. Now and then what stands on the floor shaped as a
coffer, a hutch, or a strongbox is a **mimic**: a thing that sits in the dark looking like a
chest until something touches it, and grows bigger the longer it sits. It looks exactly like a
real chest of its kind, in sight and in memory, and look mode says the same words of it.

A chest laid on a floor is a mimic by a small chance of its band. A
mimic is always of the floor's level, as a chest is, and is named by the same ten bands: a
**mimicling** passes for a coffer, a **young mimic** for a chest, an **eldest mimic** for a
gilded ark. It carries exactly what the chest would have held, and drops it all, whole, when it
dies: nothing in it is ever smashed or burnt.

## Finding one out

A mimic does nothing until it is found out: it never moves, never wakes on its own, and no
other creature takes any notice of it. It is found out when

- **you touch it**: walk into it, open it, kick it, or pick its lock. It lurches ("The coffer
  lurches: it is a young mimic!") and strikes you at once, a free blow that may hold you fast.
  Your action is not done and takes no time; the next move is yours.
- **you cast Knock on it**: it lurches, but no blow comes; the mana is spent.
- **anything strikes it**: a blow, an arrow or a thrown dagger (which stop at it), a fireball,
  a trap's burst ("The coffer shudders: it is a young mimic!").
- **you find it by searching**: it is a hidden thing like a secret door, its difficulty
  12 + level ÷ 5 (and the branch's addition per depth); in passing only if you notice
  everything (trained in Spot, or an elf). "You notice the coffer breathing: a young mimic!"

Until then it has seen no one, so a thief's sneak attack lands on it. Once found out it is an
ordinary hostile creature of the vermin and never disguises itself again. It is slow (speed
800): you can walk away from it.

## Talents

- **Mimic-wise** (scholar; Spot 10 or Intelligence 12): within two cells you see a mimic for
  what it is: it shows as itself, not as a chest. It is still disguised: it waits, unaware,
  until something finds it out.
- **Slick** (finesse; Dexterity 13): a mimic's grip never holds you.
