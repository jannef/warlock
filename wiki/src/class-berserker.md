## How to play

You fight up close with a great axe or a great sword, whichever you are trained in, and you
have the most [hit points](hit-points.md#hit-points) of any class. When a real fight starts, use
[Rage](abilities.md#ability-rage) from the [abilities menu](abilities.md#the-abilities-menu) (`a`):
while you hold [rage](rage.md) you are stronger and tougher, but you can neither read nor cast.
Being hit and killing keep it going. When it runs out you are
[Exhausted](status-list.md#status-exhausted) for two and a half minutes, so save it for the
fights that matter, and [rest](resting.md) after.
