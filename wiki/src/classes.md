# Classes

Your class is the last choice of a new run (see [Your character](character.md)): what you did
before you set out. For now a class changes your starting [stats](stats.md), adds to your [hit
points](combat.md#hit-points) (see [Combat](combat.md#hit-points)), trains you in a handful of
[skills](skills.md#who-trains-what) (and sets how many skill rolls each level gives and which
skills it is known for, see [Getting better](skills.md#getting-better)), and gives you your first ranks in a few
[weapon skills](weapon-skills.md) (a berserker has learned one of two) and
[fighting styles](fighting-styles.md) (a thief or an ascetic one of two), and decides most of your
[starting kit](starting-kit.md); no [spells](magic.md) come with it. A class with a level table also gives
**[class abilities](talents.md#class-abilities)** as you reach its levels, and bonus [talents](talents.md) (see
[Levels](#levels) below).

The first eleven are adventurers. The last four are everyday folk: an [aristocrat](classes.md#aristocrat), a [commoner](classes.md#commoner), a
[craftsperson](classes.md#craftsperson), and a [merchant](classes.md#merchant), who were never trained for this and mostly show it.

{{#include generated/classes.md}}

The changes are added to the average score of 10, together with your [race](races.md)'s, your gender's, and
your [birth sign's](horoscope.md#what-a-sign-does). Each class rolls dice for its key stats, and sometimes for a
weakness too: `1d3+1` is two to four, `−1d2` takes away one or two (see
[How your stats add up](character.md#how-your-stats-add-up)). Classes are not balanced against
each other, and some are poor choices on purpose.

## Levels

Each class's level table gives an ability at levels 1, 6, 12, 18, 25, 32, 40, 50, and 60, and
a bonus [talent](talents.md) at a few levels between, from the trees it names. The
[bard](classes.md#bard)'s is shorter: it stops at level 30 (see [The bard](#the-bard) below),
with its abilities at 1, 3, 6, 9, 12, 16, 20, 25, and 30. An ability comes the moment
you reach its level ("You gain Fencing."), and works from then on; in the game each is told in
words, here exactly. A stat it raises rises for good, score and potential. Some abilities are
used from the [abilities menu](abilities.md); the rest simply work. So far the [warrior](classes.md#warrior), the
[berserker](classes.md#berserker), the [paladin](classes.md#paladin), the [ascetic](classes.md#ascetic), the [priest](classes.md#priest), the [druid](classes.md#druid), the [wizard](classes.md#wizard), the [witch](classes.md#witch), the [ranger](classes.md#ranger), the
[thief](classes.md#thief), the [bard](classes.md#bard), and the everyday folk have their tables. A table may hand out an ordinary talent
(the [ranger](classes.md#ranger)'s and the [thief](classes.md#thief)'s [Evasion](talents.md#talent-evasion), the [bard](classes.md#bard)'s [Fast healer](talents.md#talent-fast-healer), the [druid](classes.md#druid)'s [Beast-friend](talents.md#talent-beast-friend)); if you took it already, the level gives
you a talent pick in its place.

A [paladin](classes.md#paladin)'s holy abilities used from the menu ([Lay on hands](abilities.md#ability-lay-on-hands), [Smite](abilities.md#ability-smite) and its modes, [Holy radiance](abilities.md#ability-holy-radiance))
work only while you are on the good side, Kind or better ([Consecrate](abilities.md#ability-consecrate) Neutral or better; see [Alignment](alignment.md)):
otherwise you can still use them, and nothing happens (no [zeal](resources.md#resource-zeal) spent). The passives do not care, but for
[Retribution](talents.md#talent-retribution), which answers no blow outside them. [Smite](abilities.md#ability-smite) and
[Lay on hands](abilities.md#ability-lay-on-hands) are paid in [zeal](resources.md#resource-zeal), not [mana](mana.md#how-much). The [berserker](classes.md#berserker)'s [rage](abilities.md#ability-rage) runs on a pool of its own, [Rage](resources.md#rage), and the
[ranger](classes.md#ranger)'s [rapid shot](talents.md#talent-rapid-shot) and [volley](abilities.md#ability-volley) on [focus](resources.md#resource-focus) (see [Resources](resources.md)); the
[thief](classes.md#thief)'s [sneak attacks](talents.md#talent-sneak-attack) build [openings](resources.md#resource-openings), which [low blow](abilities.md#ability-low-blow) and [master thief](abilities.md#ability-master-thief)'s finisher spend; the
[ascetic](classes.md#ascetic)'s [flurry](talents.md#talent-flurry), [stunning strike](abilities.md#ability-stunning-strike), and [quivering palm](abilities.md#ability-quivering-palm) run on [ki](resources.md#resource-ki), and the [bard](classes.md#bard)'s [inspire](abilities.md#ability-inspire) spends
[inspiration](resources.md#resource-inspiration).

The spells a class gives (the [priest](classes.md#priest)'s, [druid](classes.md#druid)'s, [wizard](classes.md#wizard)'s, and [witch](classes.md#witch)'s few, a [bard](classes.md#bard)'s, a
[paladin](classes.md#paladin)'s, or a [ranger](classes.md#ranger)'s one or two; see [What your class gives](magic.md#what-your-class-gives))
are used from the menu as **spells** ([Magic](magic.md)): each costs [mana](mana.md#spending-it) by its [spell level](magic.md#spell-levels) and your armour, and is
greyed out ("too little") while you have less; there is no other wait. A [burst](magic.md#shapes) flies and goes
off as a [scroll](scrolls.md)'s does, a [line](magic.md#shapes) runs through everyone on it, its blows and kills
yours; a [hex](magic.md#shapes) falls on a [hostile](creatures.md#factions) you see within its reach. [Call lightning](abilities.md#ability-call-lightning) answers only under the
open sky. The [druid](classes.md#druid)'s calls bring wolves, bears, and elementals to fight beside them
as [allies](allies.md).

**The witch's cackle** ([Cackle](abilities.md#ability-cackle), from level 6) keeps her hexes on.
It costs no [mana](mana.md#spending-it) and takes the time of a spell: every hex she laid herself
([hexed](status-effects.md#status-ill-wished), [weakened](status-effects.md#status-weakened),
[sluggish](status-effects.md#status-sluggish), [drowsy](status-effects.md#status-drowsy),
[webbed](status-effects.md#status-webbed)) on a creature she sees grows a degree, up to its
maximum, and its degree's time starts again; one already at its maximum only starts again.
Another's hex and a creature out of sight are untouched, and with no creature in sight under a
hex of hers she does not cackle at all (no time is spent). The cackle is loud, a voice of
[loudness](hearing.md#how-loud) 60, louder than talk: whoever [hears it](hearing.md#creatures-hear-you)
comes to look.

**The witch's malison** ([Malison](talents.md#talent-malison), from level 18) is a passive. A
creature under a hex (anyone's) takes 3 more [damage](combat.md) from her blows, shots, and
spells for each hex it is under, before its [resistances](damage-types.md#armour-and-resistance). And when a
creature dies under a hex she laid herself, whatever killed it and wherever it is, she gains
[mana](mana.md) equal to half its level (rounded down, at least 1), never past her maximum:
"The hex comes home."

**The priest's prayer** ([Orison](abilities.md#ability-orison), from level 1) costs no [mana](mana.md#spending-it) and takes the time of a spell:
it answers the worst thing that ails you, as far as your prayers reach ([wounds](prayer.md#prayer-wounds) at first; [hunger](prayer.md#prayer-hunger),
[disease](prayer.md#prayer-disease) and [poison](prayer.md#prayer-poison), [hexes and holds](prayer.md#prayer-release), [stat damage](prayer.md#prayer-restoration), and [mana](prayer.md#prayer-mana) as the levels come; two answers a
prayer at 40, everything at 60), under a hidden [strain](prayer.md#strain) that makes prayers too close together
fizzle. A priest fallen to Callous or worse ([alignment](alignment.md)) is answered by the dark powers instead, with a
twist: the sicknesses and poisons they cure pass to the nearest [creature](creatures.md) you see. See
[Prayer](prayer.md) for every use, the names, the strain, and [the dark powers' answers](prayer.md#the-holy-and-the-dark).

**[Consecrate](abilities.md#ability-consecrate)** (the [priest](classes.md#priest)'s at level 9, the [paladin](classes.md#paladin)'s at 12) works only while you are Neutral
or better: a minute's prayer, for 15 [mana](mana.md#spending-it), that lifts a cursed, draining, or haunted room's curse for good
(see [Special rooms](special-rooms.md#cursed-rooms)).
Its dark twin, **[Communion with the dark](talents.md#talent-communion)** (the priest's at level 9), works only
while you are Callous or worse: standing in a [cursed](special-rooms.md#room-cursed),
[draining](special-rooms.md#room-draining), or [haunted](special-rooms.md#room-haunted) room, or on a level such a curse lies over,
your prayers add no [strain](prayer.md#strain), and the place's drain ([Ebbing](status-effects.md#status-ebbing)) never touches you.

The priest's **[Sanctuary](abilities.md#ability-sanctuary)** (level 50, 30 [mana](mana.md#spending-it), a minute's kneeling) hallows the ground about them
for an hour: hostile creatures keep off it and whoever stands on it heals and gets mana back
three times as fast, until the priest strikes from it (see [Special rooms](special-rooms.md#hallowed-ground)).

The [wizard](classes.md#wizard)'s **[Knock](abilities.md#ability-knock)** (from level 4, 5 [mana](mana.md#spending-it)) springs the lock of the [locked door](doors.md#locked-doors) or chest in a
direction; nothing opens by itself (see [Chests](chests.md#locked-chests)).

The [wizard](classes.md#wizard)'s **[Scribe](abilities.md#ability-scribe)** (from level 12) writes a scroll of a spell he knows on a
blank scroll, for three times the spell's [mana](mana.md#spending-it) and a study's time (see
[Scrolls](scrolls.md#writing-scrolls)): mana banked between fights, read in the next. His
**[Magister](talents.md#talent-magister)** comes at level 40.

The [ranger](classes.md#ranger)'s **favoured prey** is the kind of [creature](creatures.md) you have killed most, counted over every
[level](experience.md#levels); when two are tied, the one that got there first stays favoured until another is killed
more often. From level 18 **[Pack tactics](talents.md#talent-pack-tactics)** makes you and your
[companion](allies.md#companions) hunt as one: a creature standing beside you both is
**flanked**, and your melee attacks and your companion's at it get +2 [to hit](combat.md#hitting)
(not shots, throws, or spells), and your companion's attacks at your favoured prey carry your
favoured prey's bonus, to hit and damage ([Master hunter](talents.md#talent-master-hunter)'s too). From level 40 **[Sic 'em](abilities.md#ability-sic)** sets your companion on a creature you see for 5 [focus](resources.md#resource-focus): it [hunts that creature, savage](allies.md#companions). A **[volley](abilities.md#ability-volley)** looses three shots of your [readied missile weapon](ranged.md#getting-ready) at the place you [aim](controls.md#aiming)
at, one after another, for the time of one ordinary shot; it stops early when you run out, and
costs 6 [focus](resources.md#resource-focus). **[Rapid shot](talents.md#talent-rapid-shot)** gives you focus (10, full); every shot you loose while you have
at least 2 spends 2 and takes 10 % less [time](turns.md#what-actions-cost), and with less it is an ordinary shot.

The [ascetic](classes.md#ascetic)'s **[martial arts](talents.md#talent-martial-arts)** gives you [ki](resources.md#resource-ki) at level 1, full (5 with
[Willpower](stats.md#stat-willpower) 12, growing with Willpower and your [level](experience.md#levels)), and **[flurry](talents.md#talent-flurry)**, from level 1
too, spends it: every attack with nothing in your hands strikes a
second blow, at −2 [to hit](combat.md#hitting), for a point of ki; with none left, the attack is a single blow.
**[Step of the wind](abilities.md#ability-step-of-the-wind)** (level 6) makes your steps 10 % quicker in no body armour and with no
shield, and for a point of ki, taking no time at all, leaves you [light-footed](status-effects.md#status-light-footed): your next action,
whatever it is (a step, a blow, a shot), takes half its [time](turns.md#what-actions-cost). It cannot be stacked: while you are light-footed
already it is refused and costs nothing.
**[Patient defence](abilities.md#ability-patient-defence)** (level 18) costs a point of ki and the [time](turns.md#what-actions-cost) of a wait, and leaves you
[poised](status-effects.md#status-poised) until your next action, whatever it is: +4 [Dodge](combat.md#defence), and every melee blow you dodge (it misses
by your Dexterity or Dodge) or parry meanwhile is answered with a blow of yours at once, a
[riposte](combat.md#free-blows). Taken again while poised, it holds for one action more; with any grip, a staff too.
**[Stunning strike](abilities.md#ability-stunning-strike)** (level 12) costs 1 ki: a blow of
your bare hand that leaves a foe it wounds [stunned](status-effects.md#status-stunned), caught
[flat-footed](surprise.md#stunned) and slow to act. It cannot be used with a weapon in hand.
**[Stillness](abilities.md#ability-stillness)** (level 25, with [still mind](talents.md#talent-still-mind)) costs 2 ki and
takes no time at all: whichever of these you suffer ends at once, so you act this very turn:
[held](status-effects.md#status-held), [pinned](status-effects.md#status-pinned), [staggered](status-effects.md#status-staggered), [stunned](status-effects.md#status-stunned),
[surprised](status-effects.md#status-surprised), [transfixed](status-effects.md#status-transfixed), [frightened](status-effects.md#status-frightened), [drowsy](status-effects.md#status-drowsy), and
[webbed](status-effects.md#status-webbed). With none of them on you it is refused and costs nothing. Any grip will do.
**[Diamond soul](talents.md#talent-diamond-soul)** (level 40) adds +2 [Deflection](combat.md#defence), and while you hold no weapon and no
shield, a [shot or a throw](ranged.md) that would hit you, a [trap](traps.md)'s arrow too, is plucked from the air for a point of ki,
spent by itself: the hit is a miss, and the missile falls at your feet. With no ki left, or a weapon or shield in hand, it hits. Spells and
blasts are never caught.
**[Quivering palm](abilities.md#ability-quivering-palm)** (level 50) costs 5 ki and has no other wait; it cannot be used with a weapon in hand.

### The bard

The bard knows a little of everything and masters nothing: a wandering singer who has handled
every weapon once, picked up every trade a little, and sings. Their table is shorter than the
others': it gives its last ability at level 30 and stops there. A bard learns nothing new past
30; experience earned after it still counts, but buys no level, and what comes after is found,
not given (see [Prestige classes](#prestige-classes)). The bard's two
**[songs](abilities.md#songs)**, **[inspire](abilities.md#ability-inspire)** (level 9, paid in
[inspiration](resources.md#resource-inspiration), lifting you) and the
**[lullaby](abilities.md#ability-lullaby)** (level 20, paid in [mana](mana.md#spending-it), making
[hostile](creatures.md#factions) hearers [drowsy](status-effects.md#status-drowsy)), are told in
full under [Songs](abilities.md#songs): what they cost, how far they are heard, how
[Music](skills.md#skill-music) lengthens them, and the odds of shaking the lullaby off. A
[virtuoso](talents.md#talent-virtuoso) (level 30) sings for nothing, a degree longer.

**[Travelled hands](talents.md#talent-travelled-hands)** (level 3) raises every
[weapon skill](weapon-skills.md) and [fighting style](fighting-styles.md) the bard has at rank 0 to rank 1, once (never a
[school of magic](schools.md), and never a hidden style): a point surer with whatever comes to hand.
**[Jack of all trades](talents.md#talent-jack-of-all-trades)** comes in two ranks, at levels 12
and 25: the first trains every [skill](skills.md) the bard
lacks (but Sneak attack, the thief's) at 1d4+1, whatever skills there are; from then on they are
skills like any other, used, marked, and raised at a level. The second makes every skill count 5
higher. A class table may give a talent rank by rank like this; a rank missed comes with the next.

## Prestige classes

There is no multiclassing: you keep the class you chose for good. But a class that stops short
of the rest, as the [bard](#the-bard) does at 30, may later open onto a **prestige class**: a
second table of levels, reached only by a **calling**, a deed found somewhere in the world, never
by experience alone. The game never names one; the world leaves hints, such as a
[statue](terrain.md#walls)'s inscription.

- **Your level is your total.** A bard of level 30 called into a prestige class becomes
  Hunter 1 at level 31, and Hunter 30 at 60. The [status bar](controls.md#the-screen) shows the
  total; the [character screen](character.md) shows both, `Bard 30, Hunter 4`.
- **The cap.** Each class has a last level (the [table](experience.md#levels) says which): past
  it your [experience](experience.md) still counts but buys no level. A calling raises your cap by
  the prestige class's levels.
- **The calling** is open to a character of a class it calls from, at the level it asks (that
  class's last), with no prestige class yet. Walking through it calls you at once: you are told so,
  and if you have less experience than the next level costs you are given exactly what you lack, so
  your first prestige level comes at once (experience banked past your old cap buys every level
  it can, all at once, too).
- **What carries across**: everything. Your class's abilities, talents, skills, hit points, and
  [mana](mana.md) stay as they were. Each level from then on is the prestige class's: its
  [hit points](combat.md#hit-points), its mana, its [skill rolls](skills.md#getting-better) and the
  skills it is known for, its bonus [talents](talents.md), and its abilities, at its own levels.
  What a level gives by your total still holds (no hit points past 50, no mana past 60; see
  [What a level gives](experience.md#what-a-level-gives)), and so does the experience your race
  and first class need.

The **Hunter** has sworn the old oath and been answered: a fighter whose weapon comes when
called. **[Spirit weapon](abilities.md#ability-spirit-weapon)**, from Hunter 1, calls a weapon of
spirit-stuff into your hand in the shape of any melee weapon you choose, for the time of wielding
one and nothing else: a plain weapon of that kind in every way (its damage, weight, and grip),
+3 [to hit](combat.md#hitting), with a [threat](combat.md#criticals) 2 lower, ignoring every
point of the target's [Protection](combat.md#protection). It fades the moment it leaves your
hands, and cannot be thrown (see [Spirit weapon](abilities.md#ability-spirit-weapon)).
