# Classes

Your class is the last choice of a new run (see [Your character](character.md)): what you did
before you set out. For now a class changes your starting [stats](stats.md), adds to your [hit
points](combat.md#hit-points) (see [Combat](combat.md#hit-points)), trains you in a handful of
[skills](skills.md#who-trains-what) (and sets how many skill rolls each level gives and which
skills it is known for, see [Getting better](skills.md#getting-better)), and gives you your first ranks in a few
[weapon skills](weapon-skills.md) (a barbarian has learned one of two) and
[fighting styles](fighting-styles.md) (a thief or a monk one of two), and decides most of your
[starting kit](starting-kit.md); no [spells](magic.md) come with it. A class with a level table also gives
**[class abilities](talents.md#class-abilities)** as you reach its levels, and bonus [talents](talents.md) (see
[Levels](#levels) below).

The first eleven are adventurers. The last four are everyday folk: an [aristocrat](classes.md#aristocrat), a [commoner](classes.md#commoner), a
[craftsperson](classes.md#craftsperson), and a [merchant](classes.md#merchant), who were never trained for this and mostly show it.

{{#include generated/classes.md}}

The changes are added to the average score of 10, together with your [race](races.md)'s, your gender's, and
your [birth sign's](horoscope.md#what-a-sign-does). Each class rolls dice for its key stats, and sometimes for a
weakness too: `1d3+1` is two to four, `−1d2` takes away one or two (see
[How your stats add up](character.md#how-your-stats-add-up)). Classes are not balanced against
each other, and some are poor choices on purpose.

## Levels

Each class's level table gives an ability at levels 1, 6, 12, 18, 25, 32, 40, 50, and 60, and
a bonus [talent](talents.md) at a few levels between, from the trees it names. An ability comes the moment
you reach its level ("You gain Fencing."), and works from then on; in the game each is told in
words, here exactly. A stat it raises rises for good, score and potential. Some abilities are
used from the [abilities menu](abilities.md); the rest simply work. So far the [warrior](classes.md#warrior), the
[barbarian](classes.md#barbarian), the [paladin](classes.md#paladin), the [monk](classes.md#monk), the [priest](classes.md#priest), the [druid](classes.md#druid), the [wizard](classes.md#wizard), the [witch](classes.md#witch), the [ranger](classes.md#ranger), the
[thief](classes.md#thief), the [bard](classes.md#bard), and the everyday folk have their tables. A table may hand out an ordinary talent
(the [ranger](classes.md#ranger)'s and the [thief](classes.md#thief)'s [Evasion](talents.md#talent-evasion), the [bard](classes.md#bard)'s [Lore](talents.md#talent-lore)); if you took it already, the level gives
nothing in its place.

A [paladin](classes.md#paladin)'s holy abilities used from the menu ([Lay on hands](abilities.md#ability-lay-on-hands), [Smite](abilities.md#ability-smite), [Holy radiance](abilities.md#ability-holy-radiance), [Consecrate](abilities.md#ability-consecrate))
work only while you are on the good side, Kind or better (see [Alignment](alignment.md)):
otherwise you can still use them, and nothing happens. The passives do not care. [Lay on hands](abilities.md#ability-lay-on-hands) drains you instead of costing
[mana](mana.md#how-much). The [barbarian](classes.md#barbarian)'s [rage](abilities.md#ability-rage) runs on a pool of its own, [Rage](resources.md#rage), and the
[ranger](classes.md#ranger)'s [rapid shot](talents.md#talent-rapid-shot) and [volley](abilities.md#ability-volley) on [focus](resources.md#resource-focus) (see [Resources](resources.md)); the
[thief](classes.md#thief)'s [sneak attacks](talents.md#talent-sneak-attack) build [openings](resources.md#resource-openings), which [low blow](abilities.md#ability-low-blow) and [master thief](abilities.md#ability-master-thief)'s finisher spend; the
[monk](classes.md#monk)'s [flurry](talents.md#talent-flurry) and [quivering palm](abilities.md#ability-quivering-palm) run on [ki](resources.md#resource-ki), and the [bard](classes.md#bard)'s [inspire](abilities.md#ability-inspire) spends
[inspiration](resources.md#resource-inspiration) when the bard has enough.

The spells a class gives (the [priest](classes.md#priest)'s, [druid](classes.md#druid)'s, [wizard](classes.md#wizard)'s, and [witch](classes.md#witch)'s few, a [bard](classes.md#bard)'s, a
[paladin](classes.md#paladin)'s, or a [ranger](classes.md#ranger)'s one or two; see [What your class gives](magic.md#what-your-class-gives))
are used from the menu as **spells** ([Magic](magic.md)): each costs [mana](mana.md#spending-it) by its [spell level](magic.md#spell-levels) and your armour, and is
greyed out ("too little") while you have less; there is no other wait. A [burst](magic.md#shapes) flies and goes
off as a [scroll](scrolls.md)'s does, a [line](magic.md#shapes) runs through everyone on it, its blows and kills
yours; a [hex](magic.md#shapes) falls on a [hostile](creatures.md#factions) you see within its reach. [Call lightning](abilities.md#ability-call-lightning) answers only under the
open sky. A [priest](classes.md#priest)'s
[Smite](abilities.md#ability-smite) is the [paladin](classes.md#paladin)'s, and works only while the priest is Kind or better.

**[Consecrate](abilities.md#ability-consecrate)** (the [priest](classes.md#priest)'s at level 9, the [paladin](classes.md#paladin)'s at 12) works only while you are Kind
or better: a minute's prayer, for 15 [mana](mana.md#spending-it), that lifts a cursed, draining, or haunted room's curse for good
(see [Special rooms](special-rooms.md#cursed-rooms)).

The [wizard](classes.md#wizard)'s **[Knock](abilities.md#ability-knock)** (from level 4, 5 [mana](mana.md#spending-it)) springs the lock of the [locked door](doors.md#locked-doors) or chest in a
direction; nothing opens by itself (see [Chests](chests.md#locked-chests)).

The [ranger](classes.md#ranger)'s **favoured prey** is the kind of [creature](creatures.md) you have killed most, counted over every
[level](experience.md#levels); when two are tied, the one that got there first stays favoured until another is killed
more often. A **[volley](abilities.md#ability-volley)** looses three shots of your [readied missile weapon](ranged.md#getting-ready) at the place you [aim](controls.md#aiming)
at, one after another, for the time of one ordinary shot; it stops early when you run out, and
costs 6 [focus](resources.md#resource-focus). **[Rapid shot](talents.md#talent-rapid-shot)** gives you focus (10, full); every shot you loose while you have
at least 2 spends 2 and takes 10 % less [time](turns.md#what-actions-cost), and with less it is an ordinary shot. The [bard](classes.md#bard)'s
**[inspire](abilities.md#ability-inspire)** costs 3 [inspiration](resources.md#resource-inspiration) when you hold 3, else 3 [mana](mana.md#spending-it), and can be sung again while it
lasts (it starts over); a virtuoso's costs nothing at all, and keeps the inspiration.

The [monk](classes.md#monk)'s **[flurry](talents.md#talent-flurry)** gives you [ki](resources.md#resource-ki) (5, full): every attack with nothing in your hands strikes a
second blow, at −2 [to hit](combat.md#hitting), for a point of ki; with none left, the attack is a single blow.
**[Quivering palm](abilities.md#ability-quivering-palm)** costs all 5 ki and has no other wait.
