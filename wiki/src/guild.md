# The Weyfarers' Gilde

The **Weyfarers' Gilde** is Cēaptūn's adventurers' guild: the folk who do the work the reeve
cannot pay for and the sisters will not touch. Its hall, the **Gildhalle**, is a small timber
hall in the south-east corner of [the town](ascmearc.md), beside Factor Wulfrun's office, with its
door on the lane and the bounty board nailed up beside it. Inside, **Wat One-Eye**, the
guildmaster, an old delver who has stopped going down, hands out the work.

## The chain

Wat gives one job at a time, as a [quest](quests.md): chat with him (`C`) and ask about it. Each
job is offered once the one before it is done, and nothing else stands in the way: not your
level, not your [alignment](alignment.md) (the board does not care whose hands are dirty), not
any other quest. What each job asks for is [drawn](quests.md#drawn-quests) as Wat gives it,
and stands until it is done. Kills count only from the giving on.

1. **A bounty**: kill one creature of a kind drawn from those that live in Cealdhlāw,
   Fennmynster, and Fyrdelf (never a named monster), of the dungeons you can get into when Wat
   gives it. 40 gold, 60 experience.
2. **The hundred**: kill 100 creatures of one sort, drawn from kobolds, goblins, the undead,
   vermin, marsh things, duergar, and fire things, of those living in a dungeon you can get
   into. Any kind of the sort counts, anywhere; the quest log lists the kinds. 250 gold, two red
   potions, 500 experience. A little good.
3. **A scroll for the Gilde**: bring Wat a scroll of a kind drawn from those found as loot. It
   leaves your pack when you hand it in; one taken from a shop unpaid stays on that shop's bill.
   200 gold, 150 experience.

The chain goes on: more jobs are to come.

## Ranks

Being given the first job makes you a member: a **Tin** of the Gilde. Finishing the hundred makes
you a **Pewter**. Wat names you each time ("Wat One-Eye names you a Pewter of the Weyfarers'
Gilde."), and the character screen ([Your character](character.md)) shows the rank in the colour of its metal. A
rank is a title: the town calls you by it, and nothing else changes. There are seven, each
named after a metal and each a dearer metal than the last; the later ones wait for the jobs to
come.
