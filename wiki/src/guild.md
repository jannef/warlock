# The Weyfarers' Gilde

The **Weyfarers' Gilde** is [Cēaptūn](ascmearc.md#the-places)'s adventurers' guild: the folk who do the work the [reeve](creatures.md#creature-reeve)
cannot pay for and the sisters will not touch. Its hall, the **Gildhalle**, is a small timber
hall in the south-east corner of [the town](ascmearc.md), beside [Factor Wulfrun](creatures.md#creature-mine-factor)'s office, with its
door on the lane and the bounty board nailed up beside it. Inside, **[Wat One-Eye](creatures.md#creature-guildmaster)**, the
guildmaster, an old delver who has stopped going down, hands out the work.

## The chain

Wat gives one job at a time, as a [quest](quests.md): [chat](npcs.md#chatting) with him (`C`) and ask about it. Each
job is offered once the one before it is done, and nothing else stands in the way: not your
[level](experience.md#levels), not your [alignment](alignment.md) (the board does not care whose hands are dirty), not
any other [quest](quests.md). What each job asks for is [drawn](quests.md#drawn-quests) as Wat gives it,
and stands until it is done. Kills count only from the giving on.

1. **A bounty**: kill one creature of a kind drawn from those that live in [Cealdhlāw](dungeons.md#the-dungeons),
   [Fennmynster](dungeons.md#fennmynster), and [Fyrdelf](dungeons.md#fyrdelf) (never a named monster), of the dungeons you can get into when Wat
   gives it. 40 [gold](shops.md#money), 60 [experience](experience.md).
2. **The hundred**: kill 100 creatures of one sort, drawn from [kobolds](creatures.md#creature-kobold), [goblins](creatures.md#creature-goblin), the undead,
   vermin, marsh things, duergar, and fire things, of those living in a dungeon you can get
   into. Any kind of the sort counts, anywhere; the [quest log](quests.md#the-quest-log) lists the kinds. 250 [gold](shops.md#money), two red
   [potions](objects.md#class-potions), 500 [experience](experience.md). [A little good](alignment.md).
3. **A [scroll](scrolls.md) for the Gilde**: bring Wat a scroll of a kind drawn from those found as loot. It
   leaves your [pack](objects.md#your-pack) when you hand it in; one taken from a [shop](shops.md) unpaid stays on that shop's bill.
   200 [gold](shops.md#money), 150 [experience](experience.md).
4. **Wanted**: kill one of four wanted criminals, drawn as Wat gives it. Each has gone to ground
   at the bottom of a lair of four floors, a [hidden place](overworld.md#hidden-locations) that Wat marks on your map as he gives
   the job; the other three lairs stay hidden for the rest of your life. A named monster: only
   their death counts, however it came and whoever dealt it. 500 [gold](shops.md#money), 800 [experience](experience.md). [Good.](alignment.md)

The chain goes on: more jobs are to come.

## The wanted

The four are people of the town's own day, known by what the broadsheets call them. Each lair
is four floors deep, of [danger](dungeons.md#who-lives-there-and-how-many) 6 on its first floor to 9 on its last (see
[Dungeons and danger](dungeons.md#the-lairs-of-the-wanted)), and the criminal waits in a room of
their own on the fourth, asleep until something wakes them. Each carries a [unique item](magic-items.md#unique-items) of their
own (see [Magic items](magic-items.md)), and drops it where they fall.

| Criminal | Crime | Lair | Where | Carries |

| [Mother Sallow](creatures.md#creature-sallow), the Fennhām Poisoner | sold poison to heirs; three dead at one wedding feast | Wyrtholm, the herb islet: caves under an islet | Micelfenn, east of the river | Widowmaker |
| [Black Wystan](creatures.md#creature-wystan), the Gentleman of the Road | highwayman of the Fennhām road; killed a carter for his boots | Wulfeshol, the wolf's hole: caves | Deorcholt, in Lindwudu | High Toby |

## Ranks

Being given the first job makes you a member: a **Tin** of the Gilde. Finishing the hundred makes
you a **Pewter**, and bringing down your wanted criminal an **Iron**. Wat names you each time ("Wat One-Eye names you a Pewter of the Weyfarers'
Gilde."), and the character screen ([Your character](character.md)) shows the rank in the colour of its metal. A
rank is a title: the town calls you by it, and nothing else changes. There are seven, each
named after a metal and each a dearer metal than the last; the later ones wait for the jobs to
come.
