# The Weyfarers' Gilde

The **Weyfarers' Gilde** is [Cēaptūn](ascmearc.md#the-places)'s adventurers' guild. Its folk do
the work the [reeve](creatures.md#creature-reeve) cannot pay for and the sisters will not touch.

Its hall, the **Gildhalle**, is a small timber hall in the south-east corner of
[the town](ascmearc.md), beside [Factor Wulfrun](creatures.md#creature-mine-factor)'s office. Its
door is on the lane, with the bounty board nailed up beside it. Inside,
**[Wat One-Eye](creatures.md#creature-guildmaster)**, the guildmaster, hands out the work. He is
an old delver who has stopped going down.

## The yard

Across the lane from the hall's door is the Gilde's yard, a square of beaten earth with four
[training dummies](weapon-skills.md#target-dummies): one at the west end of the top row, to shoot
at down the yard, and three in the row below, to strike. Anyone may use them.

## The chain

Wat gives one job at a time, as a [quest](quests.md). [Chat](npcs.md#chatting) with him (`C`) and
ask about it. Each job is offered once the one before it is done, and nothing else stands in the
way: not your [level](experience.md#levels), not your [alignment](alignment.md) (the board does
not care whose hands are dirty), and not any other quest. What each job asks for is
[drawn](quests.md#drawn-quests) as Wat gives it, and stands until it is done. Kills count only
from the moment the job is given.

1. **A bounty**: kill one creature of a kind drawn from those that live in
   [Cealdhlāw](dungeons.md#the-dungeons), [Fennmynster](dungeon-guide.md#fennmynster), and
   [Fyrdelf](dungeon-guide.md#fyrdelf), never a named monster. Only the dungeons you can get into
   when Wat gives it count, and a [Beast-friend](talents-exploring.md#talent-beast-friend) (a
   [druid](class-druid.md)) is never given an animal ([drawn quests](quests.md#drawn-quests)). Pays 40 [gold](shops.md#money) and 60 [experience](experience.md).
2. **A score and five**: kill 25 creatures of one sort, drawn from
   [kobolds](creatures.md#creature-kobold), [goblins](creatures.md#creature-goblin), the undead,
   vermin, marsh things, duergar, and fire things, among those living in a dungeon you can get
   into; a Beast-friend never draws a sort with an animal among its kinds (the vermin). Any kind
   of the sort counts, anywhere, and the [quest log](quests.md#the-quest-log) lists
   the kinds. Pays 250 gold, two red [potions](objects.md#class-potions), and 500 experience.
   [A little good](alignment.md).
3. **A [scroll](scrolls.md) for the Gilde**: bring Wat a scroll of a kind drawn from those found as
   loot. It leaves your [pack](objects.md#your-pack) when you hand it in. One taken from a
   [shop](shops.md) unpaid stays on that shop's bill. Pays 200 gold and 150 experience.
4. **Wanted**: kill one of four wanted criminals, drawn as Wat gives it. Each has gone to ground
   at the bottom of a lair four floors deep, a [hidden place](overworld.md#hidden-locations) that
   Wat marks on your map as he gives the job. The other three lairs stay hidden for the rest of
   your life. The criminal is a named monster, so only their death counts, however it came and
   whoever dealt it. Pays 500 gold and 800 experience. [Good.](alignment.md)
5. **Eleven floors down**: reach the eleventh floor of [Grundlēas](dungeon-guide.md#grundlēas),
   the endless stair in the grass south of the Beorhtburna road. You must get past the tenth floor
   and whatever keeps it. Reaching it once is enough. Pays 600 gold and 900 experience.
   [A little good](alignment.md).

More jobs are to come.

## The wanted

The four are people of the town's own day, known by what the broadsheets call them. Each lair is
four floors deep, from [danger](dungeons.md#who-lives-there-and-how-many) 6 on its first floor to
9 on its last (see [The lairs of the wanted](dungeon-guide.md#the-lairs-of-the-wanted)). The
criminal waits in a room of their own on the fourth floor, asleep until something wakes them.
Each carries a [unique item](magic-items.md#unique-items) of their own (see
[Magic items](magic-items.md)) and drops it where they fall.

## Ranks

Being given the first job makes you a member: a **Tin** of the Gilde. After that:

- finishing a score and five makes you a **Pewter**;
- bringing down your wanted criminal makes you an **Iron**;
- standing on the eleventh floor of Grundlēas makes you a **Steel**.

Wat names you each time ("Wat One-Eye names you a Pewter of the Weyfarers' Gilde."), and the
character screen ([Your character](character.md)) shows the rank in the colour of its metal.

A rank is only a title: the town calls you by it, and nothing else changes. There are seven
ranks, each named after a metal dearer than the last. The later ones wait for jobs still to come.
