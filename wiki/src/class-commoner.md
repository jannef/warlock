## How to play

You are the plainest of folk: a club, a few turnips, and twelve copper coins. Levels come
faster for you than for any other class, and your bonus [talents](talents.md) may come from any
tree, so you can grow into anything. [Pack mule](talents-body.md#talent-pack-mule) lets you carry more.
Buy a weapon and armour in Cēaptūn as soon as you can, and take the easy dungeons slowly.
