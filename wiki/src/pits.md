# Hellegeat, the Pits

**Hellegeat**, the hell-gate, is not a place on any map. It is the fiends' own dungeon, and no
stair, road, or quest leads there. A Pit opens only when a [miscast](miscasts.md#gates) tears the
floor open, and every such opening is a Pit of its own. Pits can open in any world you play.

## How a Pit opens

One of the four worst kinds of miscast splits the floor beside the caster. Stairs go down into the
Pit, and a [gate](miscasts.md#gates) stands at their head, letting fiends through. A creature that
miscasts can open one too.

A Pit opens anywhere but on the overworld and on a Pit's own floor: in a town, in a dungeon, even
on a floor of [Grundlēas](dungeon-guide.md#grundlēas). Where it cannot open, the gate comes alone.
There is no limit to how many Pits a run can have.

The stairs stay where they opened. Climb out of the Pit's first floor and you come back up those
stairs, however you reached that floor. A Pit opened on a floor that forgets, such as Grundlēas's,
is forgotten with that floor. Go down into it and the floor above is forgotten, stairs and all:
climbing out brings you to a new floor, with no way back into that Pit.

A Pit has five floors of hot, red-lit caves (45 °C). Its [traps](traps.md) are
[fireballs](traps.md#trap-fireball) and [pits](traps.md#trap-pit). Two floors in five hold
[chests](chests.md), one or two each. It has no [shops](shops.md) and no
[special rooms](special-rooms.md).

## Who lives there

Fiends, and only fiends: from [lemures](creatures.md#creature-lemure) and
[dretches](creatures.md#creature-dretch) up to [vrocks](creatures.md#creature-vrock),
[nycaloths](creatures.md#creature-nycaloth), and the [erinyes](creatures.md#creature-erinyes).
Demons and devils hate each other, so they fight on these floors as readily as they fight you
(see [Factions](creatures.md#factions)). Nothing [strays](dungeons.md#strays) into a Pit, and
nothing that lives there strays out.

A Pit grows with you. Its first floor is [danger](dungeons.md#who-lives-there-and-how-many) 12,
and each floor down adds 4. On top of that comes half of every level you have past 10, at most 30
more. The danger is read when a floor is made, at your level then.

## The warden

The fifth floor is the last. It leads nowhere deeper. Its hall belongs to the
[warden of the gate](creatures.md#creature-gatewarden), a level-50 fiend with claws and a barbed
whip that can [hold](status-effects.md#status-held) you. Every two minutes it tears open a new
gate, a [rift](creatures.md#creature-rift) or a [hellmouth](creatures.md#creature-hellmouth), two
at most at once, and what comes through fights for it. The Pit's other fiends leave it alone.
A gate your own [miscast](miscasts.md) opens is different: the fiends it lets through attack the
warden too. The
hall has lava in its corners, four locked [chests](chests.md), and a hoard of finds.

Every Pit has its own warden. Killing one **seals** that Pit, and only that Pit. Make the fifth
floor again after that and the hall is still there, with a new hoard, but no warden.

A sealed Pit's stairs close behind you. The next time you arrive anywhere outside every Pit, its
mouth crumbles to rubble, and that Pit is gone for good. You are never shut inside: the stairs
close only once you are out.

## Floors that forget

A Pit's floors forget, as [Grundlēas](dungeon-guide.md#grundlēas)'s do. Leave a floor and
everything on it is gone, your memory of it too. Climb back up and the floor above is made again:
the same caves, but its dead are back and its loot is new. A floor pays
[experience](experience.md#discovery) for arriving only the first time you reach that depth of
that Pit.

Your [allies](allies.md) come with you as anywhere else. Those too far away stay behind, and that
is the last of them. Your [companion](companions.md#companions) or
[familiar](familiars.md#familiars) is never left behind.
